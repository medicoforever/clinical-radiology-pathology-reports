### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1661`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / MRI PNS`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

Few scattered areas of centriacinar nodules noted in the medial segment of right middle lobe and superior segment of right upper lobe and the inferior lingular segment of the left upper lobe.
Mild varicoid type of bronchiectasis predominantly central in location noted involving both lungs, some of the dilated bronchi showing soft tissue density within - suggestive of mucus plugs.

Few enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes noted in the upper para-oesophageal, precarinal, subcarinal and right hilar regions, largest one measuring 1.3 cm in short axis.  No evidence of necrosis/ calcification within.

Even distribution of the pulmonary arterial branches noted.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Haemangioma noted in the D10  and D12 vertebral bodies.
A coronal cleft noted in the D7 vertebral body.

IMPRESSION: Known case of bronchial asthma. Present scan shows:
"	Bilateral central varicoid bronchiectasis with mucus plugging with few scattered centriacinar nodules in both lungs as detailed
"	Mediastinal lymphadenopathy.
Possibility of the allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis to be considered.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `6277`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / MRI PNS`  

```text
LIMITED MRI SCAN OF BRAIN & ORBITS

Technique : FLAIR axial, T2 f/s coronal, axial, T1 coronal.
.                   Post contrast - T1 f/s axial, coronal f/s.

5.2 x 2.7 x 2.4cm (AP x TR x CC) lobulated well defined uniformly enhancing lesion is noted in medial aspect of right orbit, extending from the preseptal region upto the orbital apex.  Superior rectus muscle is displaced superiorly and at the orbital apex level it is not separately seen from the lesion.  Medial rectus muscle is not seen separately from the lesion at the level of orbital apex.  The lesion is displacing the globe anterolaterally.  Optic nerve is displaced medially by the lesion.  The lesion appears hypointense in both T1 and T2 WI.  Focal bony defect is noted in roof of right orbit and in superior orbital rim.  No abnormal meningeal enhancement seen.

Rest of the orbital structures are normal.

Age related cortical atrophy is seen.

Scattered subcortical and deep white matter T2 / FLAIR hyperintensity is noted in bilateral corona radiate and centrum semiovale.

Mucosal thickening with air fluid level noted in bilateral maxillary sinuses with occlusion of both maxillary ostium - suggestive of acute sinusitis. Mucosal thickening is also noted in both the frontal sinuses suggestive of sinusitis.

IMPRESSION: 

1.	Uniformly enhancing intraconal lesion in the medial aspect of right orbit causing proptosis appearing hypointense in both T1 and T2 with focal bony defect as detailed- possibly inflammatory mass of fungal etiology / ?? metastasis.
SUGGESTED: Histopathology correlation.

DR.N.SUMATHI / DR.ARUNKUMAR
```
