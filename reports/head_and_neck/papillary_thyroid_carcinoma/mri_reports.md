MRI SCAN OF NECK AND MEDIASTINUM
Clinical profile: Known case of papillary carcinoma of thyroid, post thyroidectomy, now for evaluation of residual disease:
Technique:  Dixon - T2 axial, T1 axial, T2 haste coronal, axial and sagittal, and post contrast vibe T1 fat sat T1 all three planes.  
Mediastinum:
A large moderately and heterogenously enhancing mass lesion measuring approximately 7 x 6.1 x 4.3 cm (CC x TRA x AP) is seen in anterior mediastinum, extending from just above the suprasternal notch region to lower border of D6 vertebral level. The lesion has multiple focal T2 hypointense areas, which corresponds to calcification and hemorrhages. (With CT correlation). 
The fat plane around the lesion is clearly visualised in the lateral aspects and is not clearly delineated in the posterior and anterior aspects. The lesion is in close contact with anterior margin of arch of aorta and anterior borders of proximal parts of the great arteries with loss of fat plane. The contact area of lesion is about 3.3 cm with the arch of the aorta. The lesion appears to be minimally extending between the SVC, brachiocephalic and left common carotid arteries in the anterior aspect. No significant encasement/ narrowing of caliber of aorta or great vessels seen in the present study. The possibility of infiltration has to be considered.
The lesion causes mass effect over the great veins of the mediastinum. Horizontal part of the left brachiocephalic vein is not seen separately. The possibility of severe compression/ invasion by the mass has to be considered. Few venous collaterals are seen in the left side of the mediastinum. 
Fat stranding with few small enhancing nodules are seen also seen in the superior aspect of the mass lesion. 
No abnormal thickening or enhancement of pericardium/ pericardial effusion seen now.
Rest of the visualised mediastinal structures appears normal. No other significant sized lymphadenopathy seen. Pulmonary vessels appear normal. 
No significant focal lesions seen in the visualised lung fields. (With CT correlation). 
Neck:
Thyroid gland is not visualised consistent with surgery with fat stranding. Mild irregular patchy enhancement is seen in the thyroid bed region on both sides (L>R), with mild nodularity on left side, largest nodule measuring 8.4 x 5.2 mm. Similar patchy enhancement is also seen in left anterior aspect of neck anterior to thyroid cartilage. No other significant focal lesion seen.
Few discrete lymph nodes are seen in bilateral level II positions, largest measuring 5.2 mm in short axis at left level II position. 
Rest of the neck structures appears normal.
IMPRESSION: 

"	Large enhancing mass lesion in anterior mediastinum with extensions, mass effect and altered fat planes as detailed. The possibility of invasion to be considered. 
"	Post-operative changes in thyroid bed region with enhancing foci as detailed. 
Features are consistent with residual disease.  Suggested clinical correlation.
