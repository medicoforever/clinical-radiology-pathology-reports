FOLLOW UP  PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: - Papillary Ca thyroid , post thyroidectomy and Radioiodine ablation therapy (TENIS) , with recurrence and post PRRT ;for evaluation.

Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. Blood sugar level was checked before the scan and was 118 mg/dl.

Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
Post total thyroidectomy status.
There is a large, Intensely hypermetabolic thyroid  mass in the tracheo-esophageal groove measuring 4.5x3.4x5cms (previously 5x3.7x5cms) with an SUV max of 15 (prior  SUV max of 19). It is seen to compress and likely invade the trachea, the esophagus is not separately seen consistent with invasion.
The air containing (presumably necrotic) portion of the lesion has reduced in size.
Large, Intensely hypermetabolic, 3.9x2.1cms left supraclavicular node is seen, no necrosis, with an SUV max of 15(prior SUV max 24.8).
Multiple , moderately hypermetabolic sub-cm nodes noted in right supraclavicular, and left level I, II regions with an SUV max of 3.2. (also seen on previous scan)
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.

CHEST:
Multiple mildly hypermetabolic sub-cm nodules seen in both lungs which are peripherally placed and upper lobe consistent with metastases. Largest of these is 2.9mm in apico-posterior segment of left upper lobe with an SUV max of 0.9(prior SUV max 1.1).
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.

PERSISTENT HYPERMETABOLIC LEFT SUPRACLAVICULAR, RIGHT CERVICAL LYMPHNODES AND BILATERAL LUNG NODULES; CONSISTENT WITH METASTASES.
