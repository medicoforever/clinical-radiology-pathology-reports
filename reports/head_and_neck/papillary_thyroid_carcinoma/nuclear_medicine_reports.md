WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: - Papillary Ca Thyroid with lymphnode metastases post thyroidectomy and I-131 treatment with rising thyroglobulin and I-131 whole body scan was negative (TENIS);for evaluation.

Whole body images (base of skull of mid-thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
Moderately hypermetabolic significant soft tissue thickening is seen around the medial end of the clavicle with an SUV max of 4.5. Mild sclerosis of the medial end of left clavicle is seen.
Few discrete mildly hypermetabolic lymph nodes are seen in the bilateral level I A, I B and II positions, largest measuring approximately 8 mm in short axis in the left level I B position with an SUV max of 1.  
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
Thyroid gland is not seen, probably post-operative changes. 
Deviated nasal septum bony septal spur to left is seen.
Polypoidal mucosal thickening is seen in the bilateral maxillary sinuses, suggesting sinusitis.

CHEST:
Numerous well-defined intensely hypermetabolic pulmonary nodules of varying sizes are seen in both lung fields in a random fashion, largest measuring approximately 11.6 x 11.5 mm in the right lower lobe with an SUV max of 18.4, consistent with metastasis.
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Two ametabolic large simple cysts are seen in the left kidney, largest measuring approximately 12.3 X 10cm in the upper polar region. No significant calcification/ septations seen within.
Ametabolic calculus measuring approximately 8 mm is seen in the lower polar region of right kidney.
Ametabolic well-defined exophytic lesion measuring approximately 23 X 21 mm with density of 23 HU is seen in the lower polar region of right kidney.  Suggested contrast study for further evaluation. 
Ametabolic well-defined fat containing lesion measuring approximately 12.6 X 7.8 mm is seen in the right adrenal gland, suggestive of myelolipoma.  
Patchy increased attenuation of the mesenteric fat is seen, may represent mesenteric panniculitis. 
Mild Prostatomegaly is seen with prostatic calcification.
Phleboliths are seen on both sides of the pelvis.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.

SKELETAL:
