### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `51591`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / MRI Neck`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: MPR T1, FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, DWI,  3D TOF MRA. Post contrast images - T1 space, T1 MPRAGE

A large well-defined lobulated mass lesion measuring about 5.4 X 5 X 5.4 cm (anteroposterior X transverse X craniocaudal) is noted in the sphenoid sinus region with destruction of all walls of sphenoid sinus.  Posteriorly the lesion courses destruction of the clivus and petrous apex on both sides projecting into the prepontine cistern.  Superiorly the lesion causes destruction of the floor of the anterior and middle cranial fossa indenting over the brain parenchyma.  The sella turcica and pituitary gland is not separately visualised from the mass lesion.  Anteriorly the lesion causes destruction of the wall of posterior ethmoidal air cells and inferiorly projects into the nasopharynx.  The nasopharyngeal airway is completely obliterated by the mass lesion.The lesion encases the intracranial portion of optic nerves bilaterally (left more than right).  The mass displaces the optic chiasm superiorly The mass indents the medial aspect of the internal carotid artery on both sides, however no obvious encasement is noted.  

The lesion is predominantly isointense to grey matter on both T1 & T2-weighted images.  An area of hyperintensity noted on T1-weighted images within the tumour which appears hypointense on T2-weighted images suggestive of haemorrhage secondary to surgery.  With contrast, the lesion shows moderate inhomogeneous enhancement.  Subtle enhancement of the dura noted in the region of the floor of the anterior cranial fossa on left side.  Possibility of dural invasion cannot be ruled out.    

2.7 X 1.7 X 3 cm sized extra-axial CSF  intensity lesion noted in right parasagittal location indenting the frontal lobe.  No restriction on diffusion weighted images suggestive of arachnoid cyst.

Few scattered white matter hyperintensities in bilateral frontoparietal lobes suggestive of ischaemic changes.

The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.

The periventricular and deep white matter display normal volume and signals.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.

The imaged cranial nerves are also normal.

The cerebellum and cerebellar peduncles are also normal.

The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.

IMPRESSION: 

1.	A large heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion in the sphenoid sinus region with extensions as detailed - suggestive of malignant aetiology possibly sphenoidal sinus carcinoma. Needs histopathology correlation

2.	Arachnoid cyst - right frontal region.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `56451`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / MRI Neck`  

```text
CT SCAN OF NECK(PLAIN STUDY)

Serial axial sections taken from skull base up to aortic arch level.

A large Soft tissue density lesion noted involving the nasopharynx , predominantly on the right side.The lesion extends anteriorly into the right nasal cavity.The lesion extends inferiorly upto the base of the dens. Cranially it causes erosion of the floor of the sphenoidal sinus and the floor of the middle cranial fossa on right side. The right foramen ovale appears enlarged. Posteriorly the lesion causes erosion of the adjacent clivus. Postero laterally the lesion indents the ICA and jugular vein on right side. The parapharyngeal fat is displaced laterally. Bony destruction also noted of the medial pterygoid plate on the right side.

Multiple lymph nodes noted involving bilateral level II, III and level V nodes, largest in right level V measuring 2.2cms in short axis.Right supraclavicular node noted measuring 7mm in short axis with calcification. Another node in right level V shows calcification within 

Mucosal thickening is noted within the paranasal sinuses. 

Trachea and visualized portion of esophagus are normal.

Soft tissue density in the right middle ear cavity and the right eustachian tube. 

Few small nodular opacities, noted in the visualised portion of right lung. Visualised portion of left upper lobe shows fibrosis, calcified granulomas and bronchiectatic cavities - tuberculous sequelae. Needs clinical correlation. 

IMPRESSION: 

Soft tissue lesion in right nasopharynx with extensions and bony erosions as detailed. 

Multiple lymph nodes as detailed. 

-	Features suggestive of nasopharyngeal carcinoma with nodal metastasis. 

Needs contrast enhanced MRI for further evaluation. 

DR.PETER JEYARAJ
```

```text
MRI SCAN OF NECK

Technique : T2 Sagittal, T1, T2 axial, T2 coronal, postcontrast T1, DWI. 

Patient known case of recurrent carcinoma tongue status post operative and free tissue graft, post radiotherapy and chemotherapy.

Present scan shows postoperative changes in the form of poor delineation of fat plane and architectural distortion of oral cavity.
Postoperative changes noted in the mandible.  However no altered marrow signal intensity or enhancement noted within the mandible.
Postoperative flap noted in the right side of the tongue extending to the tongue base region. Mild mass effect noted over the pre-epiglottic and vallecular region probably due to postoperative changes.

There is a well-defined irregular enhancing area measuring 1.6 x 1.3 x 1.1 cm (transverse by anteroposterior by craniocaudal) noted along the anterolateral aspect of the left half of tongue/floor of mouth.  No obvious diffusion restriction noted in this region.

There is subtle another focal enhancing area measuring 8.8 x 5.5 mm in size near the base of the tongue on the left side.

No significant cervical lymphadenopathy noted.

The nasopharyngeal area and vocal cord region appear normal.

IMPRESSION: 
Patient known case of recurrent carcinoma tongue status post operative and free tissue graft, post radiotherapy and chemotherapy. Present scan shows,

DR SEETHARAMAN / DR MANISH
```
