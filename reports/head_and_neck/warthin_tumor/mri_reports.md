A well-defined cystic mass lesion measuring approximately 7.7 x 8.4 x 7.6 cm is seen in the left upper neck region with small area of contact with inferior aspect of superficial lobe of left parotid gland. The lesion causes mass effect in the form of medial displacement of left carotid vessels, internal jugular vein, left sternocleidomastoid muscle noted. Indentation over submandibular gland is seen. 
Few small homogeneously enhancing intra-parotid nodes and peri parotid regions, largest node measuring 11 mm noted in left side.
Multiple discrete enhancing nodes are seen in bilateral level IB, II, III and V regions with the largest node measuring 11 mm in short axis dimension noted in left level II.
Similar cystic lesion with similar imaging characteristics and nonenhancing solid focus, measuring 1.3 x 1.1 cm seen in lateral aspect of superficial lobe of right parotid gland. 
Rest of the parotid glands are normal. No evidence of duct dilatation / calcification seen. 
Focal smooth scalloping is seen in the adjacent mandible. No obvious bony involvement is seen.
No evidence of thickening/abnormal enhancement of the visualised nerves.
Epiglottis, glottis and postcricoid regions are normal.
The tongue, floor of mouth/buccal mucosa appears normal.
No definite evidence of extension or abnormal signal intensity in the visualised bones.
Bilateral myelohyoid muscles appear normal.
The nasopharynx, oropharynx and larynx appear normal.
Thyroid gland is normal.
Right submandibular and sublingual glands are normal.
Neck vessels show normal flow signal intensities.
Visualised spine:
Diffuse degenerative changes of the spine are seen with disc desiccation, marginal osteophytes and facetal arthropathy. Posterior disc osteophyte complex causing anterior thecal sac indentation was seen at multiple levels and cord indentation at C5-6 and C6-7 levels.
Altered signal density is seen in the left hemi cord at C5-6 level, suggesting cervical spondylotic myelopathy.
IMPRESSION:

"	Large cystic lesion with papillary/ polypoidal solid components in left upper neck region closely related to left parotid gland as detailed. 
"	Small similar lesion in right parotid gland. 
"	Bilateral cervical lymphadenopathy.  

Imaging features suggest the possibility of Warthin's tumor. However, in view of diffusion restriction, solid components and cervical lymphadenopathy, the possibility of malignant transformation has to be rule out. 

Suggested clinical and HPE correlation.
