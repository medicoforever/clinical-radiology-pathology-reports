### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `231506`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / US Parotid`  

```text
CT SCAN OF NECK

Clinical info: Left parotid tumour.

Serial axial sections taken from skull base up to aortic arch level.

Nasopharynx, oropharynx and hypopharynx are normal.

Larynx is normal.

Well defined isodense (to muscle) well circumscribed mass lesion measuring 1.9 x 2.2 x 2.2 cm (AP x TR x CC) is noted in superficial lobe of left parotid gland. The mass shows heterogeneous enhancement predominantly in peripheral region with nonenhancing cystic component in the centre. The lesion lies posterior to angle of left mandible and left retromandibular vessels. Fat plane is maintained between the vessels and the mass. The mass lies anterior to left mastoid and mastoid end of left sternocleidomastoid (SCM) muscle. Fat plane is maintained between the mass and SCM muscle.

Bilateral submandibular glands are normal.

Few enlarged homogenously enhancing lymph nodes are seen in bilateral submandibular and left posterior triangle region with largest node measuring 1.0 cm in left submandibular region.

Thyroid gland show two nodules in left lobe, largest measuring 8 x 7 mm. No calcification.

Neck vessels are normal.

Trachea and visualized portion of esophagus are normal.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Well defined mass lesion with heterogenous enhancement in left parotid gland as detailed - possibly Warthin's tumor.
-	Suggest FNAC.

Dr. Balaji Jeevanandham
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `357113`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / US Parotid`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF NECK

Clinical Details- Left parotid swelling
Technique:  Dixon - T2 axial, T1 axial, T2 coronal Dixon, T2 sag, DWI. 
                     Post contrast images - fat sat T1 all three planes

A well defined, rounded, solid cystic lesion, with smooth margins is noted in the superficial lobe of the left parotid gland.
The lesion measures approx. -3.3 x 3.9 x 4.2 cm ( in max. AP X TR XCC dimensions). Solid component of the lesion appears hypointense on T2WI and iso to hyper on T1WI. Cystic component which is at the more superior aspect appears T2 hyper and T1 hypointense and shows evidence of thin T2 hypointense septations within.
On DWI foci of restriction are noted in the solid and linear areas within the cystic component with predominant uniform enhancement on post contrast images.
The lesion extends from c2 TO c3 vertebral body in cranio caudal dimension.  Extrinsic indentation of the left sternocleidomastoid muscle with loss of fat planes with inferiorly. Laterally it is causing contour bulge over the skin.
A beak like extension of this lesion is noted medially extending in the deep lobe of the parotid gland. On the postero medial aspect the lesion is abutting the stylohyoid muscle, however the intervening fat planes are well maintained.
Bilateral para pharyngeal fat appears normal.
Few subcentimetric, uniformly enhancing lymph nodes are noted in the submental, submandibular region and level III largest measuring 4mm. 
No definite evidence of extension or abnormal signal intensity in the visualised bones.
Right parotid gland appears normal.
Epiglottis, glottis and post cricoid regions are normal.
The tongue, floor of mouth/buccal mucosa appears normal.
Bilateral myelohyoid muscles appear normal.
No significant cervical lymphadenopathy noted.
The nasopharynx, oropharynx and larynx appear normal.
Thyroid gland is normal.
Bilateral submandibular and sublingual glands are normal.
Neck vessels show normal flow signal intensities.
Degenreative changes are noted in the lower cervical pine with disc osteophyte comple causing indentation of the spinal cord at C5-C6 this level . 
IMPRESSION:
Left  parotid lesion  involving superficial lobe with deep lobe extension as detailed. Following differential diagnosis merits consideration-
1)	Warthin's tumor
2)	Pleomorhic adenoma with malignant conversion

Suggested HPE correlation
```
