### Case #001 (BEFUND_UBEID: `330301`)
- **Study Date:** `2015-01-09 18:05:23`
- **Modality Code:** `A-PET`
- **Clinical Indication:** `Right axillary lymphnode Biopsy showed Adeno squamous carcinoma? primary;for evaluation.`

```text
WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: Right axillary lymphnode Biopsy showed Adeno squamous carcinoma? primary;for evaluation.

Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid-thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. Blood sugar level was checked before the scan and was 108mg/dl.

Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
Right parotid gland:  Ametabolic 1.4 X 0.9 cm sized oval shaped enhancing lesion noted in the superficial lobe.
Thyroid gland is diffusely enlarged with intense FDG uptake (SUV max 12.8), suggestive of thyroiditis. 
Ametabolic calcified nodule measuring about 8 X 8 mm in left lobe of thyroid
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
There is no significant cervical or supraclavicular adenopathy. The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.

CHEST:
Intensely hypermetabolic 1.9 X 1.4cm sized heterogeneous enhancing focus is noted in the right axillary region, infiltrating the adjacent right axillary vein with an SUV max of 19.8.
5.5 X 4.6 cm sized collection is noted in the right axillary region with peripheral FDG uptake noted with an SUV max of 3.9- suggestive of postsurgical cavity.
Ametabolic 3mm sized calcified nodule in apical basal segment of left lower lobe - suggestive of granuloma.
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
No abnormalities are detected in breast or internal mammary node.
No abnormalities noted in the mediastinum and vasculature.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
Mildly hypermetabolic 3.2 X 2.6cm sized heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion noted in the posterior aspect of the interpolar region of left kidney with an SUV max of 1.8.  Left renal vein is normal.  
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Ametabolic 3 mm sized hypodense lesion in postop superior segment of right lobe of liver - possibly cyst.
There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.

IMPRESSION:
MILDLY HYPERMETABOLIC HETEROGENEOUSLY ENHANCING MASS LESION IN INTERPOLAR REGION OF LEFT KIDNEY AS DETAILED; SUGGESTIVE OF MALIGNANT AETIOLOGY. NEEDS HPE CORRELATION.

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC HETEROGENEOUS ENHANCING FOCUS IN THE RIGHT AXILLARY REGION ; SUGGESTIVE OF METASTASIS.

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC DIFFUSELY ENLARGED THYROID GLAND; SUGGESTIVE OF THYROIDITIS.

NO DEFINITE EVIDENCE OF METABOLICALLY ACTIVE DISEASE ELSEWHERE IN THE WHOLE BODY SURVEY.

AMETABOLIC ENHANCING FOCAL LESION IN RIGHT PAROTID GLAND; MAY REPRESENT PLEOMORPHIC ADENOMA/INTRAPAROTID LYMPH NODE (unrelated etiology).
```
