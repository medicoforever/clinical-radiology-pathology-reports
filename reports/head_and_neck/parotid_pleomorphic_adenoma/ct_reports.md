Carotid and vertebral arteries appear normal.
Prevertebral soft tissues are normally seen.

Visualised lung fields show a soft tissue nodule in left apicoposterior segment of upper lobe and centrilobular nodules in subpleural location of bilateral lungs.


IMPRESSION: 

"	Mucosal thickening in right vocal cord with thickened right aryepiglottic fold and collapsed and thickened right pyriform sinus.

"	Intra parotid enhancing lesions on the left side. Differentials being intraparotid nodes vs. primary parotid tumor like pleomorphic adenoma. 

"	Left lung soft tissue nodule with centrilobular lung nodules bilaterally.
