ULTRASOUND SCAN OF NECK 

Both the lobes of the thyroid show normal parenchymal echoes.
There is no mass lesion, cysts or calcification seen in both lobes of thyroid.

Visualized neck vessels are normal.

The left parotid gland is almost replaced by a well-defined hypoechoic mass lesion.  It shows heterogeneosity within and shows vascularity on colour Doppler. No evidence of calcification noted.  No other mass lesions seen.

Right parotid gland appears normal.

Bilateral submandibular gland appears normal.

 No evidence of significant cervical lymphadenopathy.

IMPRESSION: A well-defined mass lesion involving the left parotid gland as detailed.

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `68069`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / US Neck`  

```text
MRI NECK

Technique:  T2 DIXON axial and coronal, T1 DIXON axial, post contrast T1 DIXON axial, coronal and sagittal.
Clinical details:  A case of the right parotid lesion.
There is a well defined heterogenous lesion in superficial and deep lobe of right parotid gland which appears heterogeneously hyperintense on T2 and mildy hyperintense on T1 and shows a well defined hypointense wall. The lesion measures 2.7 x 2.2 x 2.6 cm (transverse by anteroposterior by craniocaudal) in size. The lesion shows peripheral enhancement on contrast injection, mild irregularity noted along the posterior inner wall of the lesion.  no evidence of restricted diffusion noted within the region.  There is mild T2 hyperintensity noted of the adjacent gland on T2 images along its inferior part.
Few enhancing small lymph nodes noted along the inferior aspect of lesion, largest measuring 6 mm in short axis.
Few lymph nodes are noted at bilateral level IB and II.  The largest lymph node measures 9 mm at the right level II.
The oro pharynx and hypo pharynx are normal.
Larynx is normal.  
The para pharyngeal and retropharyngeal spaces are normal.
The neck vessels are normal.
IMPRESSION :
"	Well defined peripherally enhancing lesion in right parotid gland as detailed. Possibility include benign lesion such as pleomorphic adenoma however in view of short duration of symptoms possibility of infective etiology such as parotid abscess cannot be ruled out.
```
