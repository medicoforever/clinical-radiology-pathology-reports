### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1644`)
**Modality Context:** `Ultrasound / CT Neck`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF NECK AND THORAX

Serial C.T.scan of the neck and thorax were studied with I.V.bolus contrast.

10.0 X 6.0 X 7.0 cm sized heterogenously enhancing mass lesion arising from the left lobe of thyroid noted extending across the midline. The lesion is displacing the trachea, larynx, hypopharynx and oropharynx to the right side. Focal narrowing of the trachea, with patent lumen measuring 7.0mm in coronal diameter in its narrowest part.  Carotid and IJV are posterolaterally displaced.The left submandibular gland is displaced anteriorly.  Inferiorly the lesion is extending up to the suprasternal notch.
Small subcentimetre sized poorly enhancing nodules noted in the isthmus and the right lobe of thyroid.
Multiple enlarged lymph nodes of varying sizes are noted in bilateral level V and the right level II regions, few of them showing foci of calcification and cystic degeneration and intense enhancement with contrast, largest measuring 17mm in short axis in the right level to region.
An enhancing upper mediastinal lymph node is also noted just below the mass lesion measuring 1.0cm in short axis.
Multiple prominent vascular channels noted in the hypopharynx.

The nasopharynx and parapharngeal spaces appear normal.
There is no evidence of mass lesion seen in these regions.
Prevertebral soft tissues are normally seen.

Atleast lung parenchymal 5 nodules noted, 3 in the right and 2 in the left lower lobe, largest one measuring 5.0mm.  The nodule in the right posterobasal segment shows calcification.  

Even distribution of the pulmonary arterial branches and that of the bronchial tree noted.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.  

There is no evidence of hilar lymphadenopathy seen.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Bones: Normal

IMPRESSION: Known case of papillary carcinoma thyroid.  Present scan shows:

1.  Large vascular enhancing mass lesion on the left lobe of thyroid with extension as detailed.
2. Bilateral level V and right level II lymphadenopathy - suggestive of metastasis.
3.  Few tiny nodules in both lungs - suggested further evaluation.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `3954`)
**Modality Context:** `Ultrasound / CT Neck`  

```text
ULTRASOUND SCAN OF THYROID

Right lobe of thyroid is enlarged, shows a well defined heterogenous hypoechoic lesion measuring 2.2x1.8 cm. Multiple punctuate echogenic foci noted within the lesion suggesting calcifications. Lesion shows prominent central and peripheral vascularity.

Small focal hypoechoic lesions also noted involving the isthmus.
The left lobe of the thyroid measures 1.2x1.3 cm, shows normal parenchymal echoes.

A right level IV cervical lymphnode noted measuring 0.5 cm in short axis. Multiple echogenic foci suggesting calcifications noted within the node.

Visualized neck vessels are normal.

IMPRESSION: 
Heterogenous hypoechoic lesion with punctuate echogenic foci in the right lobe of thyroid.
	Advise FNAC to rule out papillary carcinoma of thyroid.
```
