MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: MPR T1, FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, DWI,  3D TOF MRA. Post contrast images - T1 space, T1 MPRAGE

A large well-defined lobulated mass lesion measuring about 5.4 X 5 X 5.4 cm (anteroposterior X transverse X craniocaudal) is noted in the sphenoid sinus region with destruction of all walls of sphenoid sinus.  Posteriorly the lesion courses destruction of the clivus and petrous apex on both sides projecting into the prepontine cistern.  Superiorly the lesion causes destruction of the floor of the anterior and middle cranial fossa indenting over the brain parenchyma.  The sella turcica and pituitary gland is not separately visualised from the mass lesion. The nasopharyngeal airway is completely obliterated by the mass lesion.The lesion encases the intracranial portion of optic nerves bilaterally (left more than right).  The mass displaces the optic chiasm superiorly The mass indents the medial aspect of the internal carotid artery on both sides, however no obvious encasement is noted.  

The lesion is predominantly isointense to grey matter on both T1 & T2-weighted images.  An area of hyperintensity noted on T1-weighted images within the tumour which appears hypointense on T2-weighted images suggestive of haemorrhage secondary to surgery.  With contrast, the lesion shows moderate inhomogeneous enhancement.  Subtle enhancement of the dura noted in the region of the floor of the anterior cranial fossa on left side.  Possibility of dural invasion cannot be ruled out.    

2.7 X 1.7 X 3 cm sized extra-axial CSF  intensity lesion noted in right parasagittal location indenting the frontal lobe.  No restriction on diffusion weighted images suggestive of arachnoid cyst.

Few scattered white matter hyperintensities in bilateral frontoparietal lobes suggestive of ischaemic changes.

The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.

The periventricular and deep white matter display normal volume and signals.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.

The imaged cranial nerves are also normal.

The cerebellum and cerebellar peduncles are also normal.

The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.


IMPRESSION: 

1.	A large heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion in the sphenoid sinus region with extensions as detailed - suggestive of malignant aetiology possibly sphenoidal sinus carcinoma. Needs histopathology correlation

2.	Arachnoid cyst - right frontal region.
