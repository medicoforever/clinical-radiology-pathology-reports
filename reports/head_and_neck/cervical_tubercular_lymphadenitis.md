### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `53929`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / US Neck`  

```text
CT SCAN OF NECK

Patient has a known case of TB lymphadenitis and collection, status postop 6 months back.  Present scan shows:

Serial sections taken from skull base up to aortic arch level.

There is an ill-defined peripherally enhancing hypodense collection measuring approximately 34.4 x 26.1 x 9.0 mm in seen posterior to the lower third of the right sternocleidomastoid muscle at C7 level more in the medial aspect about 2.5 cm from the sternal insertion. There is obliteration of the adjacent fat planes with fat stranding. The collection is extending upto the subcutaneous fat plane just medial to the muscle at this level. Mild fat stranding is seen in the subcutaneous fat plane also.

The adjacent part of the lower third of right sternocleidomastoid muscle appears slightly thickened in size. No significant mass effect over the adjacent structures seen. There is no other focal collection noted.  

Few peripherally enhancing necrotic lymph nodes are noted in right level IV and right supraclavicular regions, largest measuring 1.7 x 1.0 cm in the supraclavicular region. No other evidence of cervical significant cervical lymphadenopathy. Mild perinodal inflammatory changes are seen.

Few small Lymph nodes of varying sizes are seen in the bilateral levels IA, IB, II, III and V largest measuring 5.9 mm in short axis in the left level IB position. (Insignificant by size criteria). 

Bilateral submandibular and parotid glands are normal.

Thyroid gland is normal.

Neck vessels are normal. No evidence of IJ V thrombosis. 

Trachea and visualized portion of esophagus are normal.

Pharynx and the larynx appear normal. Oral cavity appears normal. 

A pocket of air is noted in the anterior aspect of the C5 vertebral body suggestive of degenerative changes.

The visualised part of both lungs are normal.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Small collection posterior to lower third of the right sternocleidomastoid muscle with extension and necrotic lymphadenopathy as detailed. 
In view of previous TB lymphadenitis, tubercular etiology of the present lesions to be considered.
```

```text
CT SCAN OF NECK AND THORAX CONTRAST

Clinical profile: Tubercular cervical lymphadenitis on ATT - for follow up.

Serial sections of the neck and thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

Few mildly enlarged lymph nodes (4-5) with central necrosis noted in right level 4 and 5. Largest node measures 10 mm short axis in level 4 and 5.

Nasopharynx, oropharynx and hypopharynx are normal.

Larynx is normal.

Bilateral submandibular and parotid gland are normal.

Thyroid gland is normal.

Neck vessels are normal.

Trachea and visualized portion of esophagus are normal.

THORAX:

The study shows normal lung pattern with even distribution of the pulmonary arterial branches and that of the bronchial tree.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.  

There is no evidence of mediastinal / hilar lymphadenopathy seen.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Visualized abdomen shows no significant abnormality.

Bilateral adrenal glands are normal.

Visualized bones are normal

IMPRESSION: 

Known case of tubercular cervical lymphadenitis- 

"	Mild necrotic right lower cervical and axillary lymphadenopathy. No significant change in size compared with previous USG.
"	No abnormality in thorax.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `682537`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / US Neck`  

```text
ULTRASOUND SCAN OF NECK

Both the lobes of the thyroid show normal parenchymal echoes.

A heterogenous nodule measuring 0.5 x 0.4 cm is noted in left lobe of thyroid. Another similar nodule measuring 0.3 x 0.2 cm is noted in right lobe of thyroid. Mild vascularity is noted in both nodules.

The isthmus shows normal echoes.

Visualized neck vessels are normal.

Multiple enlarged discrete hypoechoic lymph nodes with loss of fatty hilum are noted at all levels in bilateral cervical region largest measuring 1.3 cm in both level V and right level IV.

A hypoechoic collection with mobile internal echoes measuring 2.8 x 2.1 x 1.5 cm (Volume- 5cc) is noted just below the angle of left mandible possibly collection secondary to suppurated lymph node.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Bilateral necrotic lymphadenopathy with suppurative lymphadenitis as mentioned likely tubercular etiology/ neoplastic.
"	Suspicious nodules in both lobes of thyroid- Suggested further evaluation.
```
