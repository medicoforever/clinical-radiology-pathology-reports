```text
DOPPLER STUDY OF LEFT LOWER LIMB ARTERIES

The patient known case of aortic dissection.

The study shows slightly altered triphasic flow in both common, superficial and profunda femoris arteries. Widening of the systolic phase noted with normal acceleration time seen.  A large systolic peak is followed by a small second systolic peak is seen.  Early diastolic reversal is normally seen.  The altered wave form is probably due to the aortic dissection flap.
The common iliac artery and the external iliac artery on left side shows similar flow pattern.
No evidence of the extension of the aortic dissection flap into the common iliac artery seen on the left side.

Intima is normally seen at all levels and shows mild atherosclerotic changes.

No evidence of stenosis or occlusion seen.

Popliteal artery is normally seen at all levels.

There is no evidence of stenosis seen.

Good flow is demonstrated in the posterior and anterior tibial arteries. 

IMPRESSION: 

1.  Mildly altered triphasic flow in left lower limb arteries probably secondary to the aortic dissection flap.
2.  Mild atherosclerotic changes in the left lower limb arteries with no haemodynamically significant stenosis/occlusion.
```
