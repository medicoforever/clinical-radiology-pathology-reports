The anterior commissure appears normal.
No obvious extension through the thyrohyoid membrane. No obvious invasion of the thyroid, arytenoid, cricoid cartilages noted. No obvious extension across the glottis.
Small ametabolic lymph nodes are noted involving bilateral level III largest measuring 5 mm involving the level III on the right side.

CHEST:
Bronchiectatic changes with scattered areas of hypermetabolic fibrosis with patchy hypermetabolic consolidation changes are noted in anterior segment of upper lobes, apical segment of right upper lobe and apicoposterior segment of left upper lobe with an SUV max of 5.2.
Hypermetabolic scattered centriacinar nodules with few showing tree in bud appearance noted involving the anterior segment of right upper lobe with an SUV max of 1.5.
Mildly hypermetabolic lymph nodes are noted in right upper, paratracheal, right lower paratracheal, aortopulmonary, precarinal and left hilar region, largest measuring 6.0mm with an SUV max of 1.9.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Ametabolic enhancing focus measuring 8x6mm is noted in the postero-inferior aspect of the spleen.
Ametabolic hypodense lesion measuring 10 X 8 mm noted in the body of the left adrenal gland (HU value 22).
Bilateral perinephric fat stranding noted. 
Diffuse atherosclerotic changes are noted involving the abdominal aorta and iliac vessels. 
Small ametabolic lymph nodes noted in the right external iliac region, largest measuring 5 mm.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. 

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.
Degenerative changes are noted in multiple cervical vertebrae, lower dorsal and few of the lumbar vertebrae.

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC SOFT TISSUE LESION INVOLVING THE RIGHT PERIFORM FOSSA WITH EXTENSION AS DETAILED; CONSISTENT WITH RECURRENT ACTIVE DISEASE.

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC BRONCHIECTATIC CHANGES, FIBROSIS AND CONSOLIDATION IN BOTH UPPER LOBES WITH CENTRIACINAR NODULES AS DETAILED; CONSIDER THE POSSIBILITY OF INFECTIVE AETIOLOGY.

AMETABOLIC HYPERDENSE FOCUS IN THE SPLEEN AND HYPODENSE LESION IN BODY OF LEFT ADRENAL; IS OF UNCERTAIN SIGNIFICANCE.RECOMMEND FOLLOWUP IMAGING.
