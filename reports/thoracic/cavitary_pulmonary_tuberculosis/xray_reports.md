Neck vessels are normal.

C.T.SCAN OF THORAX


Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.


Areas of bronchiectasis with scattered centriacinar nodules and few tree in bud nodules are noted involving the apical, anterior and posterior segment of right upper lobe.
A large bronchiectatic cavity is  noted involving the right lung apex.

Bronchiectatic changes are noted involving the medial segment of right middle lobe.

Small scattered soft tissue density nodules are noted in the anterior segment of left upper lobe.

Few lymph nodes are noted involving the paratracheal, pretracheal, aortopulmonary and prevascular region, largest measuring 8 mm in the paratracheal region on the right side.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.  

There is no evidence of hilar lymphadenopathy seen.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Visualized abdomen shows no significant abnormality.

Bilateral adrenal glands are normal.

Visualized bones are normal.

A nonenhancing hypodense lesion measuring 7x7mm is noted in segment VI of the right lobe of the liver, suggestive of cyst.

Small nonenhancing hypodense lesions are noted involving both kidneys, suggestive of simple renal cysts the largest cyst in the left kidney measures 1.7 X 1.4 cm.


Impression:

Ill-defined soft tissue thickening involving the post cricoid region with erosion of the cricoid and extensions as detailed.

Small lymph nodes involving left side of neck.

Bronchiectatic changes in right lung with scattered centriacinar nodules in both upper lobes and right upper lobe cavity  as detailed.
