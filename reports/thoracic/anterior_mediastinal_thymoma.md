### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `574`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / MRI Thorax`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF WHOLE BODY

Technique: whole body T1, T2, IR coronal, T1 Ax brain, T1 coronal whole body postcontrast.

There are multiple well defined expansile lytic lesions with associated soft tissue component involving the calvarium on both sides of them showing extradural component, largest measuring 9.2 x 6 cm (transverse X superior inferior) involving the  parietal bones predominantly on left side. The soft tissue appears mildly hyperintense on T2-weighted images and isointense on T1-weighted images, shows restriction on diffusion weighted images and shows enhancement on postcontrast images.

Another lesion with similar characteristics is noted in the body of the mandible on left side.

There is a focal area of hyperintensity in right cerebral peduncle which appears hyperintense on T2 and FLAIR, and do not show any restricted diffusion or enhancement on contrast injection.

Neck spaces appear normal. Nasopharynx, oropharynx and laryngopharynx appear normal.
No significant cervical, mediastinal or abdominal lymphadenopathy noted.
No mediastinal or retroperitoneal mass lesion noted.
The liver, spleen and both kidneys shows normal signal intensity and enhancement on postcontrast images.

Spine and the spinal cord appear normal. 
The pelvis and visualised long bones appear normal.  

No other focus of abnormal signal intensity, contrast enhancement or restricted diffusion noted,

IMPRESSION:
Multiple lytic lesions with associated soft tissue component involving the calvarium and the body of mandible on left side as detailed, may represent multifocal plasmacytoma.
Focal hyperintence lesion in right cerebral peduncle.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `623`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / MRI Thorax`  

```text
CT SCAN OF THE THORAX

There is a circumferential heterogeneously enhancing soft tissue growth involving the lower end of oesophagus for a length of approximately 11.3 cm. The maximum thickness measures approximately 2cm.  The mass is seen to involve the cardio-oesophageal junction and extending into the stomach for a length of approximately 2.5cm. The fat planes between the mass and the descending aorta are indistinct.
The mass closely abuts the adjacent liver parenchyma.
There is a large heterogeneous collection noted anterior to the growth.  It shows food particles within.  There is a thin linear tract seen to communicate from the growth to this collection. The collection is also seen to extend into the upper abdomen through the oesophageal hiatus.The IVC appears compressed by the collection.
The proximal oesophagus is mildly distended.

There are multiple scattered areas of ground-glass opacification noted in all segments of both lungs.  This may represent aspiration.
Through soft tissue nodules are seen in both the lungs.  Largest section nodule measures approximately 8  mm.
Consolidation is noted in the medial and posterior basal segments of the right lower lobe and posterior basal segment of the left lower lobe.
Mild left pleural effusion seen.
Multiple enlarged mediastinal nodes are seen in the prevascular, pre and right paraortic, aortopulmonary window, pre and subcarinal locations.  Largest node measures approximately 11 mm in the precarinal location.  Necrotic right hilar node is also seen.  A few large bilateral supraclavicular nodes are also noted.  Few nodes are seen in the gastrohepatic location, largest measuring 8 mm in short axis. 
Visualised bones are normal.
2.6 X 3.1 cm hypodense lesion is seen in segment four of the liver suggestive of metastasis.
No other obvious abnormality noted in the visualised abdomen.

IMPRESSION: Imaging features suggestive of the lower oesophageal  neoplastic growth with extensions as detailed.  There is a perforation with a large collection noted in the lower thorax extending into the upper abdomen.
Mediastinal adenopathy, liver and lung metastasis. Left pleural effusion. 
Features of aspiration in both lungs.
```
