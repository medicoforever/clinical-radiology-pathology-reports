WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: - Liver metastasis, unknown primary; for evaluation.
Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. A blood sugar levels were checked and was 182 mg/dl.
Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
There is no significant cervical or supraclavicular adenopathy. The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.
CHEST:
Intensely hypermetabolic circumferential wall thickening with luminal narrowing is noted in distal esophagus for a segment of approximately 7cm. Maximum wall thickness measuring 20mm. Fat plane with the adjacent structures are maintained. The lesion extends cranially from the level of left inferior pulmonary vein to just above the GE junction (D9 to D11 level).
Intensely hypermetabolic multiple lymphnodes are seen in left supraclavicular (2.7cm in short axis), pre-vascular, pre-tracheal (1cm in short axis) bilateral para-tracheal, sub-carinal (2.5cm in short axis), aorto pulmonary, and para-cardiac location (7mm in short axis), para-oesophageal and oesophago-gastric junction (2.1cm in short axis) with an SUV max in the range of 13 to 20.
Two small ametabolic nodules measuring 6mm each is seen in lateral basal segment of right lower lobe and anterior segment of left upper lobe respectively.
ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
Multiple intensely hypermetabolic heterogenously enhancing lesions of variable sizes are seen in both lobes of liver with an SUV in the range of 6 to 20.
Few similar intensely hypermetabolic characteristic lesions are also noted in spleen largest measuring 1.5x1.2cm with an SUV max of 11.6.
Multiple enlarged lymphnodes are seen in periportal, coeliac axis level, pre-aortic, 
para-aortic, retro-caval, para-caval, pre-caval, aorto-caval location, largest lymph node measuring 2.6cm in periportal location and 2.0cm in left para-aortic location with an SUV max in the range of 16 to 22.  
Umbilical hernia with fat as content is seen with defect measuring 2.8x2.1cm.


SKELETAL: 
Multiple foci of intense hyper metabolism noted, randomly distributed throughout the skeletal system, predominantly in marrow (no corresponding CT lesion) with an SUV max in the range of 9.7 to 32.   
Most intensely hypermetabolic lesions are in the left SI joint and L5 vertebrae.  
IMPRESSION:
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC LOWER ESOPHAGEAL SOFT TISSUE LESION CONSISTENT WITH PRIMARY MALIGNANCY.
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC LEFT SUPRACLAVICULAR, MEDIASTINAL AND ABDOMINAL LYMPH NODES SUGGESTIVE OF METASTASIS.
MULTIPLE INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC LIVER, SPLEEN AND SKELETAL LESIONS ALSO SUGGESTIVE OF METASTASIS.
