A large heterogeneous signal intensity enhancing mass lesion is noted in the right upper lobe with postoperative collapse involving the anterior segment of right upper lobe.  There is no obvious extension into the neural foramen/spinal canal is noted.  Few enlarged lymph nodes noted in the pretracheal and precarinal region, the largest measuring about 1.1 cm in short axis. No obvious rib destruction is noted

Rest of the individual vertebrae are normal in heights, morphology and bone marrow signal intensities.



IMPRESSION: 

Altered bone marrow signal intensity with collapse and epidural soft tissue component causing canal compromise and cord compression at D8 vertebral level as detailed.

Focal lesions involving D5 & D9  vertebra as detailed.
- Features suggestive of bony metastasis.
