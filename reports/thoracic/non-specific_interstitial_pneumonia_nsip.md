**Modality Context:** `HRCT Chest`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

Scattered areas of  ground-glass opacity with inter and intralobular septal thickening involving both lungs, predominately in the subpleural region.
No evidence of honeycombing.
Enlarged lymph nodes are noted in the right paratracheal, paraortic, bilateral hilar and para-oesophageal region.  Largest lymph nodal short axis measuring 1.4 cm in right hilar region. Sub centimetre sized lymph node with a tiny calcification is noted sub carinal region.
The study shows normal distribution of the pulmonary arterial branches and that of the bronchial tree.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.  

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Hypodense nodule measuring 1.7 x 1.5 cm size is seen involving the left lobe of the thyroid.

4.0 mm size calculus is seen in both kidneys.  Simple cortical cyst is noted involving both kidneys, largest measuring 2.4 x 1.5 cm size in right kidney.

IMPRESSION:

"	Scattered areas of  ground-glass opacity with inter and intralobular septal thickening involving both lungs, predominately in the subpleural region - possibility of nonspecific interstitial pneumonia to be considered.
"	Mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy as detailed.
"	Bilateral renal calculus and a simple renal cortical cysts.
"	Thyroid nodule - suggested  USG correlation.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `53935`)
**Modality Context:** `HRCT Chest`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Known case of interstitial lung disease with NSIP pattern. Present scan shows:

Serial sections plain high resolution C.T.Scan of thorax and CT chest after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast was studied.

Patchy areas of ground glass opacity with interlobular and intralobular septal thickening noted in both the lungs, predominantly involving the bilateral lower lobes, right middle lobe, anterior segments of both upper lobes and lingular segment of left upper lobe; with mild tractional bronchiectasis. There is no evidence of honeycombing/nodules/cysts/pleural effusion/consolidation.

Trachea is normal.

Few mediastinal nodes are noted in the highest mediastinal, upper paratracheal, lower paratracheal, precarinal, subcarinal, bilateral hilar and the lobar regions with mild contrast enhancement, few of the nodes show calcification. Largest lymphnode measuring 8.0mm is noted in the precarinal region. 

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.
The pulmonary artery is dilated measuring 29.5mm in diameter. Main pulmonary trunk:ascending aorta > 1.1. the right and left main pulmonary arteries are also dilated measuring 17.0mm in maximum diameter - suggestive of pulmonary hypertension. RV:LV<1.

No pleural effusion.

Visualised part of the abdomen is normal. GB - contracted.

Bones are normal.

IMPRESSION: 
"	Features consistent with interstitial lung disease pattern.
"	Few enlarged enhancing mediastinal and hilar lymphnodes.

DR.ASHITA
```
