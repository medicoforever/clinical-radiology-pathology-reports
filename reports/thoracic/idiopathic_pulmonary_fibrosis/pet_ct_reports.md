Clinical Indication: - Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis with weight loss and elevated CA 19.9 ? Malignancy ? Primary ;for evaluation.

Whole body images (base of skull of mid thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.

CHEST:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
Inter and intralobular septal thickening with traction bronchiectasis and bronchiectasis and honeycombing are seen in both lungs with more severe involvement of the bilateral lower lobes in subpleural location with mild grade FDG uptake with an SUV max of 1.1.  These changes are consistent with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.
Few ametabolic lymph nodes are seen in right to left paratracheal levels measuring 7 mm in short axis diameter.
The main, right and left pulmonary arteries are dilated.  The main pulmonary artery measures 3.5 cm, right pulmonary artery measures 2.3 cm and the left pulmonary artery measures 1.8 cm. These findings are consistent with pulmonary arterial hypertension, likely cor pulmonale.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Few small calcifications are seen in the pancreatic parenchyma. No obvious mass lesion and abnormal FDG uptake noted.
Simple cortical cysts are noted in the upper pole of both kidneys.  The cysts on the right kidney measures 2.0 cm and is exophytic.  The cyst on the left kidney measures 2.3 cm.
Extensive atherosclerotic wall calcification is noted with involvement of the origin of the celiac axis, bilateral renal arteries and inferior the centric artery.  The abdominal aorta is also tortuous in its course.
There is a calculus measuring 1.8 cm seen adjacent to the left posterolateral wall of the bladder.
Prostate is enlarged.

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.

NO DEFINITE EVIDENCE OF METABOLICALLY ACTIVE DISEASE ANYWHERE TO SUGGEST MALIGNANCY IN THE WHOLE BODY SURVEY.

IDIOPATHIC PULMONARY FIBROTIC CHANGES IN THE LUNG AND PULMONARY HYPERTENSION.

AMETABOLIC CALCIFICATION IN THE PANCREAS ; IS OF BENIGN ETIOLOGY.
