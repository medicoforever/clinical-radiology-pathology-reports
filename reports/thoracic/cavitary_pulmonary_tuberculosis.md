### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `186252`)
**Modality Context:** `HRCT / Chest X-ray`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Clinical profile: k/c/o pulmonary tuberculosis, presenting with cough.

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

The left lung shows a large thick and smooth walled cavity with fluid level , measuring 5.4 x 4.5 x 3.5 cm with the wall measuring 1.6 cm.  There are multiple branching chunky calcifications noted in the left lung - suggestive of broncholiths. Multiple branching nodules are noted in the basal segments - suggestive of plugging of the bronchioles.

There are pleural calcifications noted in the bases and posteriorly in the left hemithorax. There is patchy pleural thickening mostly in the posterior costophrenic angles. There is loss of volume in the left lung.

Right lung shows multiple areas of tree in bud pattern with plugging of few of the bronchi/bronchioles (branching thick nodules) in the right upper lobe and superior segment of right lower lobe.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.  

There are few nodes in the left hilar, subcarinal and left apical region, largest 1 cm.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Liver shows two simple cysts in the segments 6 and 2, largest 1.8 x 1.2 cm.

Bilateral small type 1 renal cortical cysts are seen.

Bilateral adrenal glands are normal.

Visualized vertebrae show degenerative changes with mild osteopenia.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Large cavitary lesion in left upper lobe with broncholiths and pleural calcifications in the left lung as detailed suggestive of sequelae of reactivated pulmonary tuberculosis and old empyema.

"	Tree in bud centrilobular nodules and impacted bronchioles noted in the right lung as detailed - suggestive of active endobronchial infection.
```
