4.0 mm size calculus is seen in both kidneys.  Simple cortical cyst is noted involving both kidneys, largest measuring 2.4 x 1.5 cm size in right kidney.

IMPRESSION:

"	Scattered areas of  ground-glass opacity with inter and intralobular septal thickening involving both lungs, predominately in the subpleural region - possibility of nonspecific interstitial pneumonia to be considered.
