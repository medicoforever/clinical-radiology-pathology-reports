TRAUMA C.T.SCAN OF THORAX AND ABDOMEN

Serial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

No mediastinal haematoma.
The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

Comminuted displaced fracture of the body of the right scapula noted.
Displaced fracture of the inferolateral glenoid rim noted.

Displaced fracture of the posterior aspect of the right 9-12 ribs noted.
Undisplaced fracture of the neck of the left first rib noted.
Displaced fracture of the posterolateral aspect of the left third and fourth noted.  
Displaced fracture of the posterior aspect of left fifth rib noted.
Displaced fractures of the neck and posterior aspects of left sixth, seventh and eighth ribs noted. 
Displaced fractures of the posterior aspect of ninth rib and the neck of 10th rib noted. 

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density. No focal or diffuse pathology is noted. Periportal oedema noted along the right branch of portal vein. The intrahepatic biliary radicles, portal and hepatic veins are normal.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and attenuation.

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall.
No evidence of any stone, sludge or mass.

Multiple lacerations noted predominantly involving the upper pole and body of spleen with capsular disruption and cut off of the respective intrasplenic arteries. There is no active arterial contrast extravasation.There is a perisplenic haematoma predominantly in the subdiaphragmatic region.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system.
The perinephric area is normal.
The adrenals are hyperenhancing.

Aorta and its branches are small in caliber, however IVC appears normal in caliber. 

The bowel loops are normal in caliber and wall enhancement.
Moderate haemoperitoneum noted. No evidence of pneumoperitoneum.
There is no free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is minimally distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

The prostate is normal.

Visualised vertebrae and pelvic bones are normal. No fractures.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Right tension pneumothorax with minimal hemothorax and probable laceration of the right lung.
"	Left moderate hemothorax.
"	Bilateral lung contusions.
"	Bilateral multiple rib fractures and right scapula fracture.
"	Shattered spleen suggestive of Grade IV splenic injury with moderate haemoperitoneum.
