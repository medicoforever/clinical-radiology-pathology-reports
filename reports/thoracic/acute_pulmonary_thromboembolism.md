### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1541`)
**Modality Context:** `CT Pulmonary Angiogram (CTPA)`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

The patient is a known case of carcinoma cervix status post radiotherapy with radiation proctitis and post reversal of the colostomy.

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density and there is no area of altered attenuation or blush with I.V.contrast.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well seen and are within normal limits.

The gall bladder, spleen and adrenals are normal.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system. Small subcentimetre simple cortical cyst is seen in the upper pole of the right kidney.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal. Bilateral external iliac and superficial femoral veins show poor opacification with contrast. No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

The rectum is diffusely narrowed with the wall thickening consistent with the changes of proctitis. There is a stricture further end of approximately 4.3 cm at rectosigmoid level.  Rest of the large bowel is distended with air.  The maximum luminal diameter is 4.5 cm.
Minimal flow noted in the pelvis and between bowel loops.

There is no obvious mass in the region of the cervix or parametrium.

There is a collection in the pocket measuring 5.4 x 3 x 1.5 cm (CC x TR X AP) in the subcutaneous plane in the right lumbar region. The intra-abdominal drain is seen in situ. 

Visualised lung bases show thrombus in the left lower lobe pulmonary artery involving subsegmental arteries.

Visualised bones show no significant pathology.

IMPRESSION: 

Known case of carcinoma cervix post radiotherapy with radiation proctitis with  post reversal of colostomy.
1.	Features of proctitis with stricture at the rectosigmoid level.
2.	Minimal acsites with a focal collection in the right lumbar region.
3.	Left lower lobe pulmonary embolism.
```

C.T.SCAN OF THORAX AND ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

Suboptimal study due to poor breath hold. 
Patchy areas of air space opacity is noted involving both lung- possibility of pulmonary haemorrhage to be considered.
Subsegmental bronchi in both lungs shows soft tissue density within - suggestive of mucous plugging.
 Mosaic perfusion is noted in both lung.
Multiple scattered subcentimetre calcified nodule is seen in both lung largest calcified nodule measuring 6 x 3 mm in size.
No filling defect is noted within the pulmonary artery to suggest pulmonary embolism.
Bilateral minimal pleural effusion (left greater than right) with subsegmental atelectasis is noted in both lungs.
Very minimal pericardial effusion is also noted.
No pneumothorax.

ABDOMEN:
Liver - diffuse heterogeneous enhancement is noted in the liver parenchyma, however no definite evidence of contusion/laceration.  Periportal oedema is noted.  No dilatation of intrahepatic biliary radicles.
Common bile duct is prominent measuring 8.0 mm.
The gallbladder,pancreas and spleen - appears normal.
Kidneys - few small subcentimetre cortical cyst are noted in both kidneys, largest measuring 9 x 8 mm in size in the left kidney.
Bladder - normal. Foleys bulb in situ.
Uterus appears grossly normal.
Orthopaedic implant is noted in right femur.
No free fluid in the abdomen.
Atherosclerotic changes noted in the   aorta.  The vessels show normal contrast opacification.
Bones - no evidence of gross fracture.  However linear undisplaced fracture cannot be commented due to poor breath hold.
