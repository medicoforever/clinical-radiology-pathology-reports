### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `5613`)
**Modality Context:** `CT Aortogram / CTA Thorax`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX WITH ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the thorax and abdomen were studied.

An intimal flap noted involving descending thoracic aorta extending from just distal to left subclavian artery till D11 vertebral body.  Ascending aorta, arch branches and abdominal aorta are normal.

Two pseudoaneurysm noted at D10-D11 level with pseudoaneurysm measuring 3.2 x 0.9cm (CC x AP) and 2.6 x 0.3cm (CC x AP) along anterior and posterior aspect of aorta.  Just distal to this there is active extravasation of the contrast noted.

There is a haematoma involving the descending thoracic aorta upto diaphragm.

Left moderate and right minimal hemothorax noted.

Left mild pneumothorax noted.

Bilateral basal subsegmental atelectasis noted.

Bilateral centriacinar emphysematous changes noted.

Trachea is central.

No evidence of pericardial effusion (hemopericardium).

Minimally displaced fracture involving lateral aspect of 4th to 7th ribs on the left side.

Spine is normal.

Abdomen:

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density. No focal or diffuse pathology is noted.

The intrahepatic biliary radicles, portal and hepatic veins are normal.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and attenuation.

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall.
No evidence of any stone, sludge or mass.

The spleen is normal with regard to size, position and morphology and displays uniform enhancement pattern.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system.
The perinephric area is normal.

The adrenals are normal on both sides.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

Minimal free fluid noted in subhepatic, perisplenic region - possibly hemoperitoneum.
No evidence of pneumoperitoneum.

Bowel - no evidence of nonenhancing segment / discontinuity.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

The prostate is normal.

IMPRESSION:  

1.	Standford's type B aortic dissection with pseudoaneurysm and active extravasation as detailed.
2.	Left moderate and right mild hemothorax and left moderate pneumothorax.
3.	Rib fractures as detailed.
4.	Minimal hemoperitoneum.
```
