### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1567`)
**Modality Context:** `CT Chest + Contrast / PET-CT`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

There is a heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion measuring 5.0 x 4.7 x 5.8 cm [ ap x tr x cc ] size noted involving the apical posterior segment of left upper lobe with abrupt cutoff of the subsegmental bronchi. No evidence of calcification. Adjacent pleural thickening and subsegmental atelectasis is also noted.
 Medially the lesion invade the mediastinum with encasement of the left proximal subclavian artery and its branches - vertebral artery,internal mammary artery and costo cervical trunk. The left proximal subclavian artery  shows 50% luminal narrowing for a length of 1.2 cm. Posteromedially the lesion invade the chest wall  with destruction of both anterior and posterior cortex of the second rib.  The lesion is also seen extending to the left neural foramina at D1-D2 and D2-D3 level. However, no large spinal canal soft tissue component noted.The lesion is also abutting the oesophagus with loss of fat plane at few areas.
Multiple varying size of nodules involving both lung is noted (at least 9 in the right lung, at least 6 in the left lung), largest nodule measuring 1.1 x 1.1 cm size seen in left upper lobe.
Paraseptal emphysematous changes are noted in both upper lobe (right > left).
Bilateral paratracheal, left upper mediastinum, upper paraaortic and  subcarinal lymph nodes are noted.  The left upper mediastinal lymph node measuring 1 cm in short axis - significant by size criteria. Rest of the lymph nodes are insignificant by size criteria.
Pulmonary artery, pulmonary vein and its branches appear normal.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.  

There is no evidence of hilar lymphadenopathy seen.

The arch of the aorta and heart appears normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Liver - small nonenhancing hypodense lesion measuring 7 x 7 mm size noted involving the segment III of the liver.  Ultrasound correlation shows anehoic cystic lesion- suggestive of  simple cyst.
Mild intrahepatic biliary radicles dilatation and common bile duct dilatation noted.  Common bile duct diameter measures 1.1cm and traced till the ampulla level.
Pancreas - pancreatic parenchyma appears normal. pancreatic duct is prominent measuring 3.7 mm.

IMPRESSION:

"	HETEROGENEOUSLY ENHANCING MASS LESION INVOLVING THE APICOPOSTERIOR SEGMENT OF LEFT UPPER LOBE WITH EXTENSION AND CHEST WALL INVASION AS DETAILED.
"	BILATERAL LUNG NODULES AND THE UPPER MEDIASTINAL LYMPHADENOPATHY.
- FEATURES ARE SUGGESTIVE OF STAGE IV- BRONCHOGENIC CARCINOMA.
"	SUGGESTED MRI FOR DETAILED EVALUATION OF SPINAL CANAL EXTENSION.
"	MILDLY DILATED INTRAHEPATIC BILIARY RADICLE WITH PROMINENT COMMON BILE DUCT AND PANCREATIC DUCT - NEEDS DETAILED STUDY IF CLINICALLY INDICATED.

DR.VENKATESH / DR.MUTHURAJAN
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `19443`)
**Modality Context:** `CT Chest + Contrast / PET-CT`

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

Heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion measuring about 5.0 x 2.7 x 3.3 cm noted involving apical segment of the right upper lobe abutting the mediastinum with abrupt cutoff of apical and posterior subsegmental bronchus. No evidence of calcification/cavitation within the lesion. The area of contact of the lesion with the mediastinum measures 5.0 cm suggestive of mediastinal invasion and the area of contact of the lesion with the posterior chest wall measures 1.6 cm .
However no evidence of thrombosis or displacement of trachea or oesophagus noted.

No evidence of nodules noted in the rest of the lung.

Few small lymph nodes noted in the pretracheal and right para tracheal region largest measuring 8.0 mm in the right paratracheal region.
 
There is no evidence of hilar or supraclavicular lymphadenopathy.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Old healed fracture involving posterior and lateral aspect of 7th and 8th ribs  and posterior aspect of 9th rib on left side.

No evidence of metastaic focus in the visualised bones.

Bilateral adrenals are normal.

IMPRESSION:  

"	Heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion involving the apical segment of the right upper lobe with abrupt cutoff of  the  subsegmental bronchus and mediastinal invasion as detailed.
"	Subcentimetre sized upper and lower right paratracheal lymphadenopathy as detailed.
Imaging features are suggestive of bronchogenic carcinoma with mediastinal invasion. Suggested further evaluation to assess the lymph nodes if indicated.
```

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

The study shows a large heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion with central necrosis, measuring 7.5 X 7.8 X 6.4 cm (CC X TR X AP) in the left apical region.   Medially, the mass abuts the aortic arch with loss of fat plane between them and irregularity of the contrast column, The fat plane between the esophagus and the mass is also interrupted. superiorly the mass breaches the pleura and extra-pleural fat to encase the subclavian artery. 
.There is no bony destruction or erosion. 
Paraseptal emphysematous changes noted in bilateral upper lobe.
There is enlarged mediastinal lymph node involving pretracheal, left paratracheal & aortopulmonary region largest measuring 1.4 cm in the aortopulmonary region.  
No evidence of in the lung nodules.
There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.
Visualised abdomen appears normal.
Degenerative changes noted in the cervical and dorsal spine.

IMPRESSION:
1.	 Heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion in the left apical region with invasion as detailed - consistent with bronchogenic carcinoma.
2.	Mediastinal lymphadenopathy as detailed.
3.	Paraseptal emphysematous changes  in both lung.

Dr.Archana.M
```
