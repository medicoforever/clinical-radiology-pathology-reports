C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN (C.T. SCAN) of 28.07.2011:

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

 There is a heterogeneous poorly enhancing mass lesion measuring11x7.7x11.4 cm size noted in the segment V and VI of the liver with extension into segment VII and VIII. Tiny area of calcification is noted adjacent to the lesion. Rest of the liver parenchyma shows normal contrast enhancement.

Heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion measuring2.7x1.9x3.2 cm size is noted involving the left adrenal gland.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well seen and are within normal limits.

The gall bladder, spleen and right adrenal are normal. Splenenculi measuring 12x14x13 mm size is noted adjacent to the spleen.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of  pelvicaliceal system. Subcentimetre simple cortical cyst is noted in right kidney.

Aorta,  and paraaortic areas are normal.  
Double IVC is noted. The left IVC is draining into the left renal vein.

There is no evidence of free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is normally seen.

Prostate appears enlarged.

No obvious bowel wall thickening is noted.

Few mesenteric lymph nodes are noted below the anastomotic site, largest nodal short axis measuring 7mm. Enlarged left gastric lymph node measuring 9 mm in short axis noted.

Multiple, subcentimetre nodules are noted in the visualised segments of bilateral lung largest measuring 7 x 6 mm in size - suggestive of metastasis.

IMPRESSION: 

Known case of adenocarcinoma colon with liver metastasis- status post right hemicolectomy.
      Present Scans  shows mass lesion involving the right lobe of liver, left adrenal gland with  
      multiple nodules in the visualised  segments of lung - suggestive of metastasis.
      Mesenteric and left gastric lymphadenopathy.
      No obvious bowel wall thickening.
      Mild prostatomegaly.

MRI SCAN OF PROSTATE AND ABDOMEN
 
Technique: T1 T2 axial, T2 sagittal/coronal, Contrast - Dynamic, T1 axial coronal
                  Sagittal f/s.
 
Case of Adenocarcinoma - Prostate (Biopsy proven).
 
Prostate gland is normal in size.

T2 hypointense focus seen in superoposterior aspect of the peripheral zone of left lobe. It measures approximately 0.8 x 1.8 x 0.9cm (AP, Trans, CC) in size. This is seen to involve the adjacent left seminal vesicle.  
On spectroscopy, the abnormal area show elevated choline and reduced citrate.
On dynamic contrast study, this area shows early contrast enhancement. 

Small ill-defined area of early enhancement is seen in the peripheral zone on the right lobe. This area does not show reduction in citrate peak on spectroscopy. 
 
No obvious pelvic or abdominal adenopathy seen. 
 
Urinary bladder wall is mildly thickened. No focal abnormality seen. 
 
8mm non-enhancing cyst in segment IVA of liver. No other focal lesion is seen.
Few cortical cysts in both kidneys. 

No focal lesion in the adrenals.
No paraaortic lymphadenopathy.
No obvious lesions in the bones to suggest metastases.
 
IMPRESSION: Biopsy proven case of Adenocarcinoma of Prostate. 
                         Abnormal nodule is seen in left lobe of prostate which involves left seminal vesicle.
                         No obvious nodal or distant metastasis.
                        Stage T3B N0 M0.
