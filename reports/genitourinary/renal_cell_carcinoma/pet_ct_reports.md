Clinical Indication: - Renal cell carcinoma; for evaluation.

Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.

DNS to left side seen.
The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.

CHEST:
Multiple intensely hypermetabolic bulky lymphnodes are seen in right axillary region, largest measuring approximately 18.5 mm in short axis, with an SUV max of 26.8.
Few mildly hypermetabolic lymphnodes are seen in left axilla largest measuring 9.6 mm in short axis, with fatty hilum, with an SUV max of 1.4.
Few ametabolic small lymphnodes are seen in bilateral lower paratracheal and prevascular regions, largest measuring approximately 6.7 mm in short axis in prevascular region.
Diffuse atheromatous changes of aorta are seen.
Subsegmental atelectasis is seen in left inferior lingular segment.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
Moderately hypermetabolic large lobulated complex lesion with mild enhancing solid components and cystic areas is seen in interpolar region of left kidney measuring approximately 7.3x6.9x4.5 cm (CCxTRAxAP) with an SUV max of 3.
The lesion is predominately exophytic in position.
Peripheral rim calcification of the lesion is seen. No frank invasion of adjacent structures seen. Bilateral few focal renal parenchymal scars are seen. Small single cortical cyst is also seen in left kidney in upper pole region.
Multiple Intensely hypermetabolic lymphnodes are seen in periportal, peripancreatic, left paraaortic, preaortic, aortocaval, retrocaval, precaval, bilateral internal, external and common iliac regions, largest measuring approximately 21.2 mm in short axis in paraaortic region, with an SUV max of 9.8.
Subtle perinodal fat stranding is seen around few of the nodes.
Few intensely hypermetabolic nodes are also seen in bilateral inguinal regions largest measuring approximately 14.2 mm in short axis on right side, with an SUV max of 9.6.
Defect measuring approximately 16x14 mm is seen in umbilical region with herniation of extra peritoneal fat.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. 

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.
Diffuse degenerative changes of spine seen.
Grade I anterolisthesis of L4 over L5 vertebrae is seen.

IMPRESSION:
MODERATELY HYPERMETABOLIC LEFT RENAL MASS LESION; WORRISOME FOR MALIGNANCY.
