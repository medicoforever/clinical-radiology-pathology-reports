IMPRESSION:

Patient a known case of renal cell carcinoma with lung metastasis. Present scan shows 

Mild diffuse cortical atrophy with white matter ischemic changes as detailed.

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `4394`)

C.T.SCAN OF THORAX WITH ABDOMEN (01.09.11)

Serial axial sections of the thorax and abdomen were studied with oral (water) and I.V.Bolus 
contrast.

Scattered paraseptal and centriacinar emphysematous changes noted in both lungs.

Subsegmental atelectasis noted in basal segments of both lungs.

Small lymphnodes are noted in precarinal and subcarinal location, largest measuring 7.3mm in short axis.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

Abdomen:

Two tiny hypodense lesions measuring 4mm respectively is seen in segment 7 and 8 of right lobe of liver.

The intrahepatic biliary radicles, portal and hepatic veins are normal.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and attenuation.

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall.
No evidence of any stone, sludge or mass.

The spleen is normal with regard to size, position and morphology and displays uniform enhancement pattern.

Right kidney:
A heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion with both solid and cystic component is noted arising from lower pole of right kidney measuring 8.5 x 6.6 x 7.2cm (CC x AP x Trans).  No evidence of renal vein thrombosis.  Minimal right perinephric fat stranding is seen.  Pelvicalyceal system is normal.  Three main renal artery is seen.  Single main renal vein noted.

Left kidney:
Few simple cortical cysts are seen, largest measuring 1.2cm in the lower pole of left kidney.  No evidence of mass lesion.  Two main renal artery is seen.  Single left main renal vein which is retro-aortic.

The adrenals are normal on both sides.

Aorta is normal.  
Multiple enlarged enhancing lymphnodes with non enhancing area within suggestive of necrosis is noted in pre-aortic, para aortic, aortocaval, retrocaval, right renal hilar, periportal location.  Largest measuring 2.5cm in short axis.  These lymphnodes cause narrowing of the IVC, however no evidence of thrombosis within the IVC and the common and external iliac vein (correlated with doppler).  A fat density lesion measuring 13 x 8.3 x 4.5cm (CC x AP x Trans) is noted between the right gluteus minimus and medius muscle and extending along the lateral aspect of upper thigh suggestive of lipoma.

The bowel loops and mesentery are normal.
There is no free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

The prostate is normal.

Abdominal wall normal.

Bones are normal.

IMPRESSION: 1. Heterogeneously enhancing mass in right kidney as detailed.
                             2. Intra-abdominal lymphadenopathy.
                                  Possibility of renal cell carcinoma with nodal metastases to be               
                                  considered.
                             3. Tiny hypodense lesions in the liver of unclear significance - suggested
                                  further evaluation for further chracterisation of lesion.

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `7978`)
**Modality Context:** `CT KUB / MRI Renal`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the thorax and abdomen were studied with oral (water) after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

THORAX:

Multiple small well defined nodules noted involving all lobes of right lung and upper lobe of left lung.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.   There is no evidence of hilar lymphadenopathy seen.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

ABDOMEN: 

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density. No focal or diffuse pathology is noted.

Mild dilatation of intrahepatic biliary radicles and CBD noted.  No evidence of any focal mass lesion.

Portal and hepatic veins are normal.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and attenuation.

The gall bladder is not visualized- post cholecystectomy.

The spleen is normal with regard to size, position and morphology and displays uniform enhancement pattern.

A well defined heterogenously enhancing mass lesion measuring 8.1 x 5.7 x 7.7cm
(TR x AP x CC) noted involving the upper pole of right kidney.  Mutliple focal non enhancing areas noted within, suggesting necrosis.  No evidence of any calcification.  Mass causing invasion of perinephrid space, gerotas fascia and right crus of diaphragm.  Loss of fat plane at places with liver and right adrenal gland seen.  No evidence of renal vein invasion or thrombosis.

Left kidney is normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system.  Few small cortical cysts noted.
The perinephric area is normal.
Left  adrenal is normal.

Multiple enlarged right paraaortic and aortocaval lymphnodes noted, largest measuring 3.0cm in short axis, displacing IVC and left renal vein anteriorly.  Delayed phase images show distortion of upper pole calyces by mass lesion.  

The bowel loops and mesentery are normal.
There is no free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

Uterus and ovaries are normal.

Lytic lesion with soft tissue component measuring 5.2 x 7.3 x 5.3cm (AP x TR x CC) noted involving left sacral ala of S1 and S2 vertebra with involvement of spinal canal and neural foramina and extension to involve the articular surface of left SI joint.

Small lytic lesions also noted in right ilium and ischium.

Subtle hypodense lesions also noted involving multiple lumbar vertebral bodies.

IMPRESSION: 

1.	Heterogenous enhancing mass lesion in upper pole of right kidney with extensions as detailed.
2.	Paraaortic and aortocaval lymphadenopathy.
3.	Multiple lytic lesions involving pelvic bones.
4.	Multiple nodules in bilateral lung fields.
Possible right renal cell carcinoma with nodal, bony and lung metastasis to be considered.

DR.SABARI ARASU
```
