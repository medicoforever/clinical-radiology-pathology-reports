BLADDER - Shows smooth contour.

RIGHT OVARY - Measures2.2X3.3X3.4cm       VOL-15cc

IMPRESSION: Imaging features suggestive of cystic endometrial hyperplasia.
 Both the ovaries appear bulky - this may represent polycystic ovaries.
