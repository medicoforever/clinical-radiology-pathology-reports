### Case #001 (BEFUND_UBEID: `29225`)
- **Study Date:** `2011-12-21 09:54:25`

- **Clinical Indication:** `- Ca cervix, post treatment; now with local recurrence and left mandible swelling; for evaluation.`

```text
WHOLE BODY PET C.T.SCAN
 Clinical Indication: - Ca cervix, post treatment; now with local recurrence and left mandible swelling; for evaluation.
Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. A blood sugar levels were checked and was 82 mg/dl.
Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
Intensely hypermetabolic, focal heterogeneous sclerotic area is seen in the body of the mandible on the left side with focal thickening and erosion of the outer cortex seen with an SUV max of 37.4. Significant periosteal reaction is seen.  Soft tissue component is seen extending on both sides of the bone, more on the anterior aspect. 
Few ametabolic lymphnodes are seen in bilateral level I, II and left level III, largest measuring 7.3mm in short axis in left level II.
Mild sclerosis is seen in the left ramus of mandible.
Small ametabolic soft tissue lesion measuring 1.7 x 0.9cm is seen in the left frontal scalp region.
The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.
CHEST:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
Few fibrotic strands are seen in left upper lobe.
Few small ametabolic lymphnodes are seen in right upper paratracheal, bilateral lower paratracheal and prevascular region, largest measuring 6mm in short axis (insignificant by size and metabolism criteria).
Small calcified lymph node is seen in right hilar region.
Mild focal pleural thickening is seen in both upper lobar regions.
ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
Intensely hypermetabolic irregular, slightly lobulated heterogenously enhancing soft tissue lesion with central necrosis is seen in the cervix region measuring approximately 57.5 x 43.6 x 41.5 mm (TRA xAP xCC) with an SUV max of 12. Significant perilesional fat stranding is seen.  The fat plane between the lesion and the urinary bladder is not seen in the right posterior aspect, may represent invasion.  The fat plane between the lesion and the rectum is also not clearly delineated in few images.
Few ametabolic lymphnodes are seen in bilateral inguinal region, largest measuring 7.9mm in short axis.
Pancreas shows nodular calcific focus in the head of pancreas region.  Main pancreatic duct is prominent measuring 6.7mm. Atrophic changes of pancreas seen. No peripancreatic collection / inflammatory changes seen.
Few ametabolic lymphnodes are seen in periportal, retrocaval and paraaortic regions, largest measuring 5.2mm in short axis (insignificant by size and metabolism criteria).
Multiple small cortical cysts are seen in both kidneys, largest measuring 2.7 x 2.5cm in left upper pole region (Bosniak type I).  The largest cyst appears to be eccentric in position. Bilateral extrarenal pelvis is seen.  The right ureter is dilated up to the right vesicoureteric junction.
SKELETAL: 
Intensely hypermetabolic mixed lytic and sclerotic areas are seen in both ala of the sacrum with an SUV max of 5.
Diffuse degenerative changes of spine seen.
Intensely hypermetabolic vertebra plana with sclerosis is seen at L2 vertebra with an SUV max of 8.7.
Intensely hypermetabolic heterogenous sclerosis with wedging is seen at L4 and L5 vertebrae with an SUV max of 4.1.
IMPRESSION:
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC UTERINE CERVICAL MASS LESION CONSISTENT WITH RECURRENT DISEASE.
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC LEFT SIDE MANDIBULAR SOFT TISSUE LESION.THOUGH RARE, METASTASIS IS A POSSIBILITY. (? second primary)

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC MULTIPLE LUMBAR VERTEBRAL AND SACRAL LESIONS; LIKELY BENIGN ETIOLOGY (OSTEOPOROTIC/INSUFFICIENCY FRACTURES).
```
