Prostate - the median lobe of the prostate is enlarged and heterogeneous in signal intensity.  There is a small well-defined T2 hypointense nodule measuring 8 x 6 mm size is noted in the right peripheral zone of the prostate.  The nodule shows early enhancement on dynamic contrast study.  However no evidence of diffusion restriction.

Bilateral inguinal hernia with small bowel and its content on the right side and large bowel as its content on the left side noted.
Suboptimal study of upper abdomen due to poor breath holding.
Liver - no obvious focal lesion noted.
Kidneys - few simple cortical cysts are noted in both kidneys, largest measuring 6.8x 6.1 cm size in the left kidney.
No other significant abnormality detected in the upper abdomen.

Impression:

1) Irregular polypoidal intraluminal mass lesion involving the lower left lateral wall of the urinary bladder with small focal discontinuity of the T2 hypointense muscle layer.  However no evidence of macroscopic perivesical soft tissue component or bony or nodal metastasis.
2) Median lobe hypertrophy of the prostate with a small T2 hypointense nodule involving the right peripheral zone of the prostate which shows early enhancement on dynamic contrast study with no diffusion restriction- suggested PSA correlation.
3) Bilateral simple renal cortical cyst.
4) Bilateral inguinal hernia - as detailed.
