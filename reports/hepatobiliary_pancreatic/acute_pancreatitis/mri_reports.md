### Case #001 (BEFUND_UBEID: `7402`)
- **Study Date:** `2011-09-21 12:58:41`
- **Modality Code:** `A-MRI`
- **Clinical Indication:** ``

```text
M.R.I SCAN OF ABDOMEN


Multiplanar MRI of the abdomen was studied with imaging sequence coronal- T2; axial- T2 with fat suppression, T1; HASTE thick slab; post contrast axial- T1

A well circumscribed cystic lesion measuring 2.9x2.8x5.6 cm noted in the retroperitoneum, appearing hypointense on T1 and hyperintense on T2W images. Cranial part of the lesion is noted between the splenic vessels anteriorly and left adrenal gland posteriorly. Lesion extending anteroinferiorly with caudal part of the lesion seen anterior to the left renal vein. Inferiorly the lesion is noted abutting the adjacent small bowel loops.

Few thin internal septations noted, measuring 2.5 mm in thickness. No evidence of any solid mural nodule. No evidence of surrounding fat stranding. Fat plane with left adrenal gland appears maintained.

Post contrast sequence shows enhancement of the peripheral rim and internal septations. 

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform signal intensity. No focal or diffuse pathology is noted.

The intrahepatic biliary radicles, portal and hepatic veins are normal. Mild prominence of CBD noted measuring 7 mm. No evidence of any focal lesion.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and signal intensity. No evidence of peripancreatic fat stranding or fluid collection. Pancreatic duct appears normal.

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall.
No evidence of any stone, sludge or mass.

The spleen is normal with regard to size, position and morphology and displays uniform enhancement pattern.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show adequate enhancement pattern.
The perinephric area is normal.
The adrenals are normal on both sides.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

The bowel loops and mesentery are normal.
There is no free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

Uterus and bilateral ovaries show normal appearance and enhancement.


IMPRESSION: 
Well circumscribed retroperitoneal cystic lesion with thin internal septations as described, suggestive of retroperitoneal lymphangioma.


DR SABARI ARASU/ DR VENKATESH
```
