Cholelithiasis with choledocholithiasis as detailed with dilated biliary radicals.

Post splenectomy status.

C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density. No focal or diffuse pathology is noted.

The portal and hepatic veins are normal.

Intrahepatic biliary radicles, common hepatic duct and common bile ducts are dilated.  Common bile duct measures about11.6mm in diameter.  Pancreatic duct dilated, measures about 4.5 mm in diameter.  1.6 X 1.3 X 1.2 cm sized focal lesion which shows marked enhancement in the arterial phase and retention of contrast in the venous and delayed phase noted in the inferior aspect of the ampulla. The lesion causes obstruction at the ampulla. No significant perilesional adenopathy.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and attenuation.

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall.
No evidence of any stone, sludge or mass.

The spleen is normal with regard position and morphology and displays uniform enhancement pattern. Enlarged in size, measures about 15.5cm.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system.
The perinephric area is normal.
The adrenals are normal on both sides.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

The bowel loops and mesentery are normal.
There is no free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

The prostate is normal.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Enhancing mass lesion in the inferior aspect of the ampulla with dilatation of billiary radicles and pancreatic duct as detailed - possibility of mesenchymal tumor of ampulla like carcinoid to be considered. Needs histopathological correlation.

"	Splenomegaly
