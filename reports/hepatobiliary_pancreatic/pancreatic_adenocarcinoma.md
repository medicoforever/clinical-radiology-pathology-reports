### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `150341`)
**Modality Context:** `Pancreatic Protocol CT / MRI`  

```text
C.T. SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density. No focal or diffuse pathology is noted.

The, portal and hepatic veins are normal.

Dilated intrahepatic biliary radicles with dilated pancreatic duct ( 6.4 mm diameter) is seen.

The contrast scan along with decubitus sections shows an ill-defined  mildly enhancing nodular thickening  in the periampullary region involving mainly the second part of duodenum  more in the medial wall, causing abrupt cut-off of the common bile duct. There is loss of fat plane of this thickening with the pancreatic head. Multiple enhancing lymph nodes are seen in the esophagogastric, pre aortic , para-aortic and left iliac regions largest measuring 7.8mm in short axis.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and attenuation.

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall with presence of multiple calculi, largest measuring 9.2 mm. No evidence of mass.

The spleen is normal with regard to size, position and morphology and displays uniform enhancement pattern.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system. Tiny multiple cortical cysts are seen in bilateral kidneys.

The right kidney shows a calculus measuring 4.4 mm in the lower pole calyx. The left kidney shows a calculus in the upperpole calyx measuring 4.7mm.

The perinephric area is normal.
The adrenals are normal on both sides.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  Generalized atherosclerotic changes are noted in the aorta and bilateral iliacs.

There is no free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

The prostate is normal.

Visualized bones show osteopenia ,osteoarthritic changes in form of osteophytes with scoliosis of upper lumbar spine with concavity to right.

Visualized lung fields show intralobular septal thickening with groundglassing in the lingular lobe of left lung and patchy consolidation in the subpleural area in the basal segment of left lower lobe.

IMPRESSION: 

-	Double duct sign with irregular nodular thickeneing of wall of second part of duodenum with loss of fat plane with pancreatic head suggestive of neoplastic etiology ,most likely duodenal carcinoma.
-	Enhancing lymphadenopathy as detailed.
-	Cholelithiasis.
-	Bilateral renal calculi.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `166736`)
**Modality Context:** `Pancreatic Protocol CT / MRI`  

```text
MRCP
Technique:  T2 HASTE , T1 VIBE Axial, T1 VIBE coronal T2 HASTE Coronal, 
Well defined heterogeneous signal intensity mass lesion measuring about 6.0 x 6.1 x 6.6cm (AP x TR x CC)  is noted arising from the body of pancreas. The lesion shows solid and cystic components. Infiltration into the adjacent anterior peripancreatic fat is noted. Distal obstruction with dilatation of pancreatic duct is noted in the body and tail of pancreas. Pancreatic duct is normal in the region of head and uncinate process. Few subcentimeter sized lymph nodes noted in the perilesion region. The lesion is having contact with the adjacent SMV for about 150 degrees. Splenic vein this region not visualised s/o thrombosis with prominent gastroepiploic collaterals. The splenic artery appears to course through the mass lesion. 
Replaced right hepatic artery is seen arising from celiac artery and left hepatic artery is arising from superior mesenteric artery.
Common bile duct is normal in caliber.
Gall bladder: Few calculi are noted,  largest measures 6mm.
Liver: Shows nodular surface. Simple subcentimetric cystic lesions noted in both lobes of liver. 
Thin rim of perihepatic fluid noted.
Spleen is mildly enlarged (splenic index measures 510).
Subcentimetric simple cysts  are  noted in both kidneys.
Rest of the visulaised abdominal organs are normal in signal intensity.
Impression:

Well-defined mass lesion with both solid and cystic components showing extensions and involvement of the adjacent vascular structures as detailed - possibility of pancreatic adenocarcinoma with cystic component to be considered. 
Nodular surface of the liver - possibility of underlying liver parenchymal disease to be ruled out.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `194306`)
**Modality Context:** `Pancreatic Protocol CT / MRI`  

```text
C.T. SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with  I.V.Bolus contrast.
 
Clinical details- known adenocarcinoma right lung, to look for metastasis.

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density. No focal or diffuse pathology is noted.

The intrahepatic biliary radicles, portal and hepatic veins are normal.

A large heterogenously enhancing mass lesion with internal non enhancing necrotic areas  measuring  6.2 x7.5x7.8cm ( ap x tr xcc)  noted between the stomach bed and spleen abutting the tail of  pancreas with adjacent mesentric fat stranding. Loss of fat plane between the tail and the mass lesion is noted. Thrombosis of distal portion of adjacent splenic vein with prominent stomach wall collaterals is noted. 

Similar lesion measuring 6.5 x 4 x 6cm  ( ap  x tr x cc)  noted in the left adrenal region.

Heterogenous enhancing mass lesion in the  inferior aspect of pancreatic head and uncinate process measuring  6.2x6x6cm ( ap x tr xcc) .The fat plane between the pancreas and the lesion is lost.

10 X 6 mm sized enhancing sessile polyp noted in the medial aspect of the gastro duodenal junction. 

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall.
No evidence of any stone, sludge or mass.

Mild splenomegaly is noted. 

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system. Few simple cortical cysts are noted in both kidneys, the largest one measuring 1.2 x1.2 cm. The perinephric area is normal.

The adrenal gland is re normal on right side.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  
The bowel loops and mesentery are normal.
There is no free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

The prostate is normal.

Left inguinal hernia with fat as its content noted. 

Visualized bones are normal.

 6x8 x5.6cm   ( cc x tr x ap)  cm soft tissue density lesion noted in the anterior and lateral basal segment of right lower lobe - consistent with the primary lung malignancy.

Large nodal mass is seen in the subcarinal and  para oesophageal region indenting the left atrium.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Large heterogenously enhancing lesions in the left adrenal,  inferior aspect of head and uncinate process of pancreas,  and between the stomach and spleen as detailed- suggestive of metastases.
"	Enhancing sessile polyp in medial aspect of gastro-duodenal junction - needs follow up /further evaluation if indicated.
```
