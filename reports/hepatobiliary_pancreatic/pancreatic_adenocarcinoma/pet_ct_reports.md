WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: - Ca pancreas with liver metastases; for evaluation.

Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid-thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. Blood sugar level was checked before the scan and was 128mg/dl.

Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
There is no significant cervical or supraclavicular adenopathy. The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.

CHEST:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
Single ametabolic sub-cm aortopulmonary window node seen with preserved fatty hilum.
Aberrant right subclavian artery seen.
No abnormalities are detected in breast, bilateral axilla or internal mammary node.
No abnormalities noted in the lungs, mediastinum and vasculature.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
There is an intensely hypermetabolic ill-defined 1.9x1.3cms mass in the head of pancreas close to ampulla causing dilation of CBD-13mm and MPD (double duct sign) with an SUV max of 14. There is proximal IHBR dilation in both lobes of liver. 
Moderately hypermetabolic peripancreatic, periportal and precaval lymph nodes noted with an SUV max of 4.7.
At least 3 intensely hypermetabolic hypodense lesions seen, in segment VII (5x3.6x4.1cms), segment VI (1.9x1.2 and 1.1cms) of the liver with an SUV max of 18.4.
Multiple gall stones seen within the lumen.
A sliding hiatus hernia is noted.
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. 

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.
Diffuse degenerative changes of spine are seen.

IMPRESSION:

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC SOFT TISSUE MASS LESION IN THE PANCREATIC HEAD ; CONSISTENT WITH PRIMARY MALIGNANCY.

MODERATELY HYPERMETABOLIC PERIPANCREATIC, PERIPORTAL AND PRECAVAL LYMPHNODES ; CONSISTENT WITH NODAL METASTASES.

MULTIPLE INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC HYPODENSE LIVER LESIONS; CONSISTENT WITH LIVER METASTASES. 

CHOLELITHIASIS.

SLIDING HIATUS HERNIA. 

NO DEFINITE EVIDENCE OF METABOLICALLY ACTIVE DISEASE ELSEWHERE IN THE WHOLE BODY SURVEY.
