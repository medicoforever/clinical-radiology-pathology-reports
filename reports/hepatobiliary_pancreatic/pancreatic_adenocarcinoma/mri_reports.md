- **Clinical Indication:** `;`

```text
MRCP
Technique:  T2 HASTE , T1 VIBE Axial, T1 VIBE coronal T2 HASTE Coronal, 
Well defined heterogeneous signal intensity mass lesion measuring about 6.0 x 6.1 x 6.6cm (AP x TR x CC)  is noted arising from the body of pancreas. The lesion shows solid and cystic components. Infiltration into the adjacent anterior peripancreatic fat is noted. Distal obstruction with dilatation of pancreatic duct is noted in the body and tail of pancreas. Pancreatic duct is normal in the region of head and uncinate process. Few subcentimeter sized lymph nodes noted in the perilesion region. The lesion is having contact with the adjacent SMV for about 150 degrees. Splenic vein this region not visualised s/o thrombosis with prominent gastroepiploic collaterals. The splenic artery appears to course through the mass lesion. 
Replaced right hepatic artery is seen arising from celiac artery and left hepatic artery is arising from superior mesenteric artery.
Common bile duct is normal in caliber.
Gall bladder: Few calculi are noted,  largest measures 6mm.
Liver: Shows nodular surface. Simple subcentimetric cystic lesions noted in both lobes of liver. 
Thin rim of perihepatic fluid noted.
Spleen is mildly enlarged (splenic index measures 510).
Subcentimetric simple cysts  are  noted in both kidneys.
Rest of the visulaised abdominal organs are normal in signal intensity.
Impression:

Well-defined mass lesion with both solid and cystic components showing extensions and involvement of the adjacent vascular structures as detailed - possibility of pancreatic adenocarcinoma with cystic component to be considered. 
Nodular surface of the liver - possibility of underlying liver parenchymal disease to be ruled out.
```
