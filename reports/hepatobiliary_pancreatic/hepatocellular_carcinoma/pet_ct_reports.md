WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN
Clinical Indication: Hepatocellular carcinoma for initial staging.
Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. A blood sugar levels were checked and was 108 mg/dl.
Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
An intensely hypermetabolic  lymph node measuring 8mm in short axis is seen in right supraclavicular location, with an SUV max of 5.2.
Multiple intensely hypermetabolic  lymphnodes are seen in bilateral level 2 largest measuring 6.5mm in short axis in bilateral level 2,with an SUV max of 4.2  
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
There is no significant cervical or supraclavicular adenopathy. The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.
CHEST:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
No abnormalities are detected in breast, bilateral axilla or internal mammary node.
No abnormalities noted in the lungs, mediastinum and vasculature.
ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
An intensely hypermetabolic ill-defined heterogenously enhancing lesion measuring approximately 4.2 x 4.6 x 3.9cm (CC x Tr x AP) is noted involving segment 5 & 8 of right lobe of liver, with an SUV max of 8.3.

Multiple mildly hypermetabolic periportal lymphnodes are seen, largest measuring 9mm in short axis, with an SUV max of 2.
Few mildly hypermetabolic lymphnodes are seen in paraaortic, aortocaval and retrocaval location, largest measuring 6.7mm in short axis in aortocaval location, with an SUV max of 2
Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis.
SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.


IMPRESSION:
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC ILLDEFINED LESION INVOLVING SEGMENT V& VIII OF RIGHT LOBE OF LIVER, CONSISTENT WITH PRIMARY MALIGNANCY.

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC RIGHT SUPRACLAVICULAR AND BILATERAL LEVEL II CERVICAL LYMPHNODES ;WORRISOME FOR DISEASE INVOLVEMENT.
MILDLY HYPERMETABOLIC PERIPORTAL AND ABDOMINAL LYMPHNODES; ARE OF UNCERTAIN SIGNIFICANCE. (LIKELY INFLAMMATORY)
