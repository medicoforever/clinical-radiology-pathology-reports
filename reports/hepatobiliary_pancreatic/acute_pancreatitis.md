### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `576`)
**Modality Context:** `CECT Abdomen`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Patient is known case of acute necrotising pancreatitis with a collection in the lesser sac and associated splenic vein thrombosis.  

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V. Bolus contrast.

The study shows normal anatomical configuration of the liver with uniform density and there is no area of altered attenuation or blush with intravenous contrast.

Only a portion of the head and uncinate process of pancreas is seen to enhance normally.
Rest of the pancreas is replaced by a relatively well-defined fluid collection which is also seen to extend superiorly into the lesser sac and region of the porta. Inferiorly the collection extends into the gastrocolic region.  There is no significant perilesional fat stranding seen.  Multiple small nodes are seen in the peripancreatic and perilesional location. 
There is no abnormally enhancing mass lesion in and around the pancreas.

The gall bladder, spleen and adrenals are normal.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.
No evidence of pseudoaneurysm seen.
The splenic vein appears narrowed just prior to its confluence with the SMV.  However, it shows normal filling with contrast.  Portal vein is normal.

There is no evidence of free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is normally seen.
Prostate appears normal.

There is an ill-defined lytic lesion with surrounding sclerosis seen in the head of the right femur.  This may represent medullary infarction.
The rest of the bones appear normal.

Bilateral lung bases are clear.

IMPRESSION: Case of acute necrotising pancreatitis on follow-up. 
                           Relatively well-defined fluid collection noted in the pancreatic and peripancreatic location as detailed. 
          		    Narrowing of the splenic vein just prior to its confluence with the superior mesenteric vein.
```
