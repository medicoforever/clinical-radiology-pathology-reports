### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1705`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / MRI Liver`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

A large well-defined mass lesion measuring 16.5 X 10.7 X 11.5 cm is noted predominantly involving segment IV of the left lobe of liver with a large exophytic component inferiorly. The lesion shows peripheral nodular enhancement on arterial phase with subsequent centripetal filling of venous and delayed phases.  Few specks of calcification noted within the lesion - s/o giant hepatic hemangioma. two small lesions showing similar enhancement characteristics is noted in segment 2 and 3 of the left lobe of liver.
Mild  intrahepatic biliary radicles dilatation noted in the right lobe of liver.  However, there CBD is normal in calibre.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well seen and are within normal limits.

The gall bladder, spleen and adrenals are normal.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of  pelvicaliceal system. Few tiny cortical cysts noted in both kidneys.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

Umbilical hernia noted with peritoneal defect measuring 1.6 X 1.5 cm with omental fat and small bowel as it is content. The small bowel loop in the hernia sac bowel wall thinning and poor wall enhancement. The proximal small bowel loops are dilated, measuring 3.5 cm in caliber - suggestive of obstruction. The distal small bowel loops and the large bowel loops are collapsed.

Mild ascites noted.

The bladder is normally seen.

Uterus and ovaries:  Normal.

Diffuse osteopenia  noted. sacralisation of  L5 vertebra noted with bilateral pars interarticularis defect at L4 level with L4-L5 grade II anterolisthesis. Wedge compression noted at D9 level.  Schmorl's node with mild wedging noted at the D12 level.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Obstructed umbilical hernia  with poor contrast enhancement and wall thinning of the intrahernial small bowel loop.
"	Mild ascites.
"	Hepatic haemangiomas with a giant hemangioma arising from the left lobe with mild intrahepatic biliary radical dilatation as detailed.
"	Diffuse osteopenia with sacralisation of  L5 vertebra and bilateral pars interarticularis defect at L4 level with L4-L5 grade II anterolisthesis. Wedge compression at D9 level.  Schmorl's node with mild wedging  at the D12 level.
```
