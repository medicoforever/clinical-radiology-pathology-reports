### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `386`)
**Modality Context:** `Triple Phase CT / MRI Liver`  

```text
ABDOMEN M.R.I  25.07.2011
Patient is a known case of hepatocellular carcinoma of the liver  status post first sitting of transarterial chemoembolisation.
MRI performed for evaluation of rising alpha fetoprotein levels.
The contour of the liver is nodular with hypertrophied caudate and left lobe of the liver consistent with cirrhosis of the liver.
T2-weighted images shows diffuse ill-defined hyperintensity in right lower lobe of liver with intervening hypointense nodules. Dynamic postcontrast study shows relatively intense  enhancement  in the right lobe of liver with enhancement in subcapsular portion of the left lobe of liver. The portal phase shows washout in the right lobe. There are multiple nonenhancing hypo to isointense nodules noted in both lobes consistent with regenerating nodules.  Enhancing nodules with washout are also noted in segment IV A and IV B of the left lobe of liver.A thin layer of pericapsular fluid noted adjacent to the ring enhancing previously embolised lesion in segment VII of liver .
Portal vein and the hepatic veins appear normal.
Gallbladder is collapsed.
No significant para-aortic lymphadenopathy noted.
The visualised spleen and pancreas appear normal.
IVC and aorta shows normal flow void.
Impression:
Cirrhosis of liver with diffuse hepatocellular carcinoma involving the right lobe of liver with enhancing lesion in the left lobe of liver as detailed.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1066`)
**Modality Context:** `Triple Phase CT / MRI Liver`  

```text
MRI LIVER

The patient known case of  hepatocellular carcinoma with tumour thrombus involving the right portal vein.
MRI  liver done to evaluate extent of the lesion. 
The liver shows nodular surface with atrophy of the right lobe.  Hypertrophy of the caudate and left lobe of the liver noted.
An area of heterogeneous enhancement noted in the arterial phase involving the segment six and extending into the segment seven of the right lobe of the liver measuring 9.5 X 9.20 X 8.0 cm (craniocaudal, transverse, anterior posterior dimension). The lesion shows a heterogeneous washout in the subsequent phases predominantly along the lower aspect of the lesion.  There are 2 other tiny enhancing foci largest measuring 1.5 X 1.3 cm noted in the segment seven of the right lobe of liver in the arterial phase  which shows washout in the subsequent phases.
All  these lesions show diffusion restriction.
Thrombus noted distending the right branch of the portal vein which is seen to extend from the lesion up to the confluence with the left portal vein.  However, the main and left portal vein are patent.

The rest of the entire liver parenchyma shows heterogeneous  enhancement in the venous phase. Inferior vena cava and the hepatic veins appear normal.

Recanalisation of the umbilical vein noted.
Few retroperitoneal collaterals noted on the left side seen draining into the portal vein at the confluence.
The spleen is mildly enlarged in size.
No free fluid in the abdomen.
No significant the periportal nodes.

IMPRESSION:

Features suggestive of parenchymal liver disease.
Focal lesion involving the right lobe of the liver with the right portal vein thrombosis
 with two adjacent  focal lesions  -  to consider the possibility of multifocal hepatocellular carcinoma.

Dr. Sumathi / Dr.Mathew Cherian
```
