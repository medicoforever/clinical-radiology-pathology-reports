Technique: PD fs Axial, Sagittal & Coronal, T1 Sagittal & Coronal, IR Coronal.

The knee joint is normal with respect to alignment and joint space.

The articulating bones are normal in outline. Hyperintensity is seen involving the anterior aspect of the lateral femoral condyle and posterior aspect of the lateral tibial condyle suggestive of marrow contusion / edema.

Moderate joint effusion is seen with fluid in the intermuscular plane in the posterolateral aspect of joint suggestive of capsular tear at that level.
 
There is a bucket handle tear of posterior horn (posterior half) of lateral meniscus and the fragment is flipped anteriorly lying posterior to anterior horn.

The medial meniscus is normal in configuration and signal intensity.

The medial and lateral collateral ligaments along with the medial and lateral patellar retinaculi are normal.

Anterior cruciate ligament shows near complete tear in its mid segment. The femoral and tibial attachment sites appear intact.

The posterior cruciate ligament is intact and normal in signal intensity.

The inter meniscal and menisco femoral ligaments are also normal.

The surrounding muscles and tendons and the neurovascular structures are normal.



IMPRESSION:

1.	Bucket handle tear involving the posterior horn (posterior half) of lateral meniscus with the flipped fragment lying posterior to anterior horn.
2.	Near complete tear of mid segment of anterior cruciate ligament.
3.	Moderate joint effusion with features suggestive of capsular tear in the posterolateral aspect of joint cavity.
4.	Bone marrow contusion / edema involving the anterior aspect of lateral femoral and posterior aspect of tibia condyles.
