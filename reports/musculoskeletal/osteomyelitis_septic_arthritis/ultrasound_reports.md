- **Modality Code:** `A-US`
- **Clinical Indication:** `LEFT`

```text
LEFT SHOULDER AND KNEE ULTRASOUND

LEFT SHOULDER 

Mild effusion is seen in the left shoulder joint. Homogenous low level echoes are seen within the fluid.  The effusion is seen extending to the biceps tendon sheath and the axillary recess.  Mild diffuse thickening of the synovium is seen.  Minimal blood flow is seen along the thickened synovium.
Significant probe tenderness seen.

No evidence of extra-articular fluid collection seen in the present study.

The visualised part of the proximal humerus and articular cartilage appears normal.

The supraspinatous, Infraspinatus, biceps and subscapsularis muscles are normal in size, shape and echotexture.

LEFT KNEE

Mild joint effusion is seen in the left knee joint with fluid seen extending into the suprapatellar bursa.  Homogenous low level echoes are seen within the fluid.  Mild diffuse thickening of the synovium seen.

Significant probe tenderness seen.

No evidence of extra-articular fluid collection seen in the present study.

The visualised part of the distal femur and proximal tibia and articular cartilage appear normal.

The quadriceps and patellar tendons appear normal in thickness and echogenicity.

The visualised muscles also appear grossly normal in thickness and echotexture.

IMPRESSION:

Mild joint effusion with low level echoes in the left knee and left shoulder joints with mild thickening of the synovium.
May represent septic arthritis.
```
