### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `22959`)
**Modality Context:** `X-Ray / MRI / CT`  

```text
WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN 

Clinical Indication: - Fibula osteoblastic osteosarcoma; for staging evaluation.
Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of toe) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. A blood sugar levels were checked and was 89 mg/dl.
Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
Bilateral symmetric foci of high grade FDG uptake is noted in the neck,
Supraclavicular and paravertebral region related to activated brown adipose tissue. There is no significant cervical or supraclavicular adenopathy. The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.
CHEST:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
No abnormalities are detected in breast, bilateral axilla or internal mammary node.
No abnormalities noted in the lungs, mediastinum and vasculature
ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
Intensely hypermetabolic node measuring 3 mm is noted in the left common iliac region with an SUV max of 3.
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.
SKELETAL: 
Intensely hypermetabolic, heterogeneously and predominantly peripherally enhancing lesion measuring 13.3 x 5.9 x 5.1cm (CC x Trans x AP) noted involving the proximal portion of right fibula with bony destruction with an SUV max of 15.3. Few areas of ossification noted within - ?new bone formation / destroyed bone. The lesion is extending up to the proximal tibio fibular joint and abutting the proximal tibia. There is loss of fat plane with lateral gastrocnemus and the muscles appear bulky.
Few intensely hypermetabolic enlarged nodes noted in the right popliteal fossa largest measuring 7mm in short axis with an SUV max of 5-6.9. 
Fusion of the right transverse process of L5 and S1 vertebra noted with mild grade FDG uptake with an SUV max of 2.4.
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. 
IMPRESSION:
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC RIGHT FIBULAR MASS CONSISTENT WITH PRIMARY MALIGNANCY.
MULTIPLE HYPERMETABOLIC POPLITEAL NODES AND SOLITARY LEFT COMMON ILIAC LYMPH NODE; LIKELY METASTASIS.
NO EVIDENCE OF METABOLICALLY ACTIVE DISEASE ELSEWHERE IN THIS WHOLE BODY SURVEY.
```
