WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: - Osteosarcoma left fronto-temporal region, post excision ; for evaluation.

Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid-thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. A blood sugar levels were checked and was 92mg/dl.

Findings:
BRAIN:
Intensely hypermetabolic subtle extra axial enhancing focus is seen in left frontal region at the operative site indenting the left frontal lobe   ,  measures about 2.5 x 3.7 cm (AP x TR  ) with an SUV max of 23. The mass appears to extend across the craniectomy site with areas of residual bony fragments in this region (correlated with MRI done on 31/01/2014). 
CSF density focus is seen in the anterior aspect of left frontal region - may represent gliosis.  
Mild diffuse age related cortical atrophic changes in the form of prominent fissures, sulci spaces noted.

HEAD and NECK: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
Few ametabolic lymph nodes are noted in bilateral level II region, the largest measuring about 7 mm in short axis (insignificant by size criteria)
The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. 

CHEST:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
Few ametabolic nodular opacities measuring less than 5 mm is noted, one in the apical segment of the right upper lobe, three in the apico posterior segment of left upper lobe. Most of them are calcified.  
Few ametabolic fibrotic parenchymal band seen in the apicoposterior segment of the left upper lobe.  Subsegmental atelectasis seen in the right middle lobe.
No abnormalities are detected in breast, bilateral axilla or internal mammary node.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.

IMPRESSION:

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC SUBTLE ENHANCING FOCUS IN LEFT FRONTAL REGION AT THE OPERATIVE SITE AS DETAILED ; CONSISTENT WITH RESIDUAL ACTIVE DISEASE.

FEW AMETABOLIC CALCIFIED NODULES IN THE UPPER LOBES WITH AREAS OF FIBROTIC PARENCHYMAL BANDS OF BOTH LUNGS AS DETAILED - MAY REPRESENT  GRANULOMA / PULMONARY TUBERCULOSIS SEQUELAE.  SUGGESTED FOLLOW-UP.

NO DEFINITE EVIDENCE OF METABOLICALLY ACTIVE DISEASE ELSEWHERE IN THE WHOLE BODY SURVEY.
