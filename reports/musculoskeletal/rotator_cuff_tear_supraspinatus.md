### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `619`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Shoulder / US`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF SHOULDER JOINT

Technique: FS PD Ax, Cor, Sag, T1,T2 cor. 

The shoulder joint is normal with respect to joint space and alignment.

Fluid noted in subcoracoid bursa.

Hyperintense signal noted in supraspinatus tendon near its humeral attachment on T1, PD and seen to intensify on T2-weighted images suggestive of near complete tear of supraspinatus tendon except a few intact anterior fibres.

The articulating bones are normal in outline and marrow signal intensities.

The muscles and tendons of the infraspinatus, subscapularis and long head of biceps are normal in morphology and signal intensities. The deltoid muscle is also normal.

The anterior glenoid labrum shows altered morphology There is no obvious tear noted.
No definite capsular or ligamentous disruption noted.

The acromio-clavicular joint is normal and the acromion is posterior sloping of type I morphology.

IMPRESSION:
Near complete tear of supraspinatus tendon except few anterior fibres.
Fluid in subcoracoid bursa.

DR. MANISH/ DR. RUPA
```
