### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `292`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Knee`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF RIGHT KNEE JOINT- 25.07.2011

Technique: PD Ax/Sag/Cor, T1 Cor, IRCor.

The knee joint is normal with respect to alignment and joint space.

No significant joint effusion noted.

The articulating bones are normal in outline and bone marrow signal intensity characteristics.

The medial meniscus is normal in thickness.  There is a vertical circumferential tear involving the inferior articular surface of the lateral third of the body, and involves one third of its thickness. Another similar tear is seen involving the superior surface with the partial tear of the superior meniscocapsular ligament.

The lateral menisci are normal in configuration and signal intensities.
No evidence of tear or degeneration.

The medial and lateral collateral ligaments along with the medial and lateral patellar retinaculi are normal.

The anterior cruciate and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact and normal in signal intensity.

The intermeniscal and meniscofemoral ligaments are also normal.
Minimal fluid is noted anterior to the inter-meniscal ligament.

There is focal loss of thickness involving the medial half of the patellar cartilage.  It appears hyperintense in its deeper half.  There is minimal hyperintensity involving the subchondral bone marrow.
 
The surrounding muscles and tendons and the neurovascular structures are normal.

IMPRESSION: Vertical tears involving the body of medial meniscus as detailed.
                            Grade 4 chondromalacia involving the medial patellar cartilage.
```
