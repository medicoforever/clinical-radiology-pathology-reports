### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1794`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / CT / X-ray`  

```text
MRI of left leg, with the knee joint:

Loss of the subcutaneous tissue noted along the anterior medial aspect of the upper left leg.
Altered signal intensity is on IR  and T2 sequence noted within the subcutaneous tissue and muscles of the entire length of leg and the visualised lower half of the left thigh suggestive of inflammatory change.
Altered bone marrow signal intensity noted within the upper half half of the tibia, head the fibula and both the condyles of the femur.  
CT correlation revealed erosions along the lateral aspect of the tibial plateau , articular surface and lateral cortex.  A few erosions also noted in the fibular head.  
Multiple air pockets are noted within the medullary canal of the upper half of the tibia with destruction of the internal  trabeculations.  Few air pockets also noted within the knee joint cavity and in the adjacent soft tissue.
On contrast administration, there is enhancement of the  lateral tibial condyle.  Peripherally enhancing focus with an air-fluid level noted in the intramedullary location of the mid portion of the tibial shaft.  
The synovium of the knee joint is thickened and shows a uniform homogeneous enhancement.  
The muscles of the leg and adjacent  soft tissue shows diffuse contrast enhancement suggestive of inflammation.  No evidence of any soft tissue collections noted.
Minimal  knee joint effusion noted.
Anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments, both meniscal and lateral collateral ligament appears normal.
Medial collateral ligament is not visualised (probably post-traumatic).

Impression:
The patient known case of diabetes with extensive soft tissue injury in the left leg with mucormycosis.
Present scan shows features suggestive of osteomyelitis of the tibia with an intramedullary abscess.
Features suggestive of arthritis of the knee joint with the soft tissue inflammations as detailed.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `5180`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / CT / X-ray`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF LUMBOSACRAL SPINE AND HIP JOINTS

Technique: Sagittal, T1, T2, IR, Axial T2, IR Coronal for SI joints, STIR and T1 coronal- hip joint, Post contrast T1 fs - axial , coronal and sagittal.

The lumbosacral spine displays a normal lordotic curvature.

Grade 1 retrolisthesis of L4 over L5 with posterior osteophytes noted.

Modic type II endplate changes of adjacent endplates of L4 and L5. 

The individual vertebrae are normal in heights, morphology and bone marrow signal intensities.

The intervertebral discs are normal in heights. Rest of the vertebral end-plates is normal.

Mild diffuse annular disc bulge of L4-L5 and L5 - S1 levels causing no significant mass effect.

The conus medullaris ends at and the cauda equine is normal in appearance.

Screening of cervical spine shows degenerative changes in the form osteophytes, uncovertebarl joint and reduction in the disc height involving lower cervical vertebrae.

Screening of dorsal spine shows mixed hemangioma in the D12 vertebral body.

Abnormal signal intensity in the form of hyperintensity on STIR and T2 and hypointense on T1 is seen involving the left half of sacrum, left sacroiliac joint, left hemipelvis, articulating segments of acetabulum. The left femur show normal marrow signals. All these areas show heterogenous enhancement on postcontrast images. Moderate left hip joint effusion is noted. Fluid is also seen in the left sacro iliac joint.

Similar signal abnormality is also seen in the left iliacus,erector spinae, pectineus, gluteus minimus,medius and maximus,pyriformis and quadrates lumborum muscles.Abscess with peripheral enhancement is seen in the iliacus, gluteus minimus, medius and erector spinae muscles. The abscess in the iliacus muscles measure 3.4 x 2.5 x 6.3 cm (TR X AP X CC)

IMPRESSION: 
1.	Imaging features are suggestive of osteomyelitis and arthritis involving the left hemipelvis, sacroiliac joint and hip joint with myositis with abscess as detailed in the text.
2.	Grade 1 retrolisthesis of L4 over L5.
3.	Diffuse annular disc bulge at L4-5 and L5- S1 causing no significant mass effect.
```
