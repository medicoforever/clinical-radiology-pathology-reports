### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `12829`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Knee`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF LEFT KNEE JOINT

Technique: PD fs Axial, Sagittal & Coronal, T1 Coronal, IR Sagittal

Patello femoral and tibio femoral joint shows marginal osteophytes, joint space reduction with cartilage loss and subchondral cystic changes, suggesting osteoarthrosis.

Mild joint effusion noted. Rupture of baker's cyst noted with fluid seen around the posterior muscular compartment

The articulating bones are normal in outline and bone marrow signal intensity characteristics.

Medial meniscus extrusion noted with tear at the posterior root attachment site. Nonvisualisation of the root noted, with a small fragment at the intercondylar notch region appearing similar in signal intensity to meniscus suggesting displaced meniscal fragment. The lateral meniscus is normal in configuration and signal intensities.

The medial and lateral collateral ligaments along with the medial and lateral patellar retinaculi are normal.

Mucoid degeneration of anterior cruciate ligament.

The posterior cruciate ligaments are intact and normal in signal intensity.

The intermeniscal and meniscofemoral ligaments are also normal.

The surrounding muscles and tendons and the neurovascular structures are normal.

IMPRESSION:
1.	Osteoarthrosis of knee joint with mild effusion.
2.	Posterior root attachment site tear of medial meniscus, with displaced meniscal fragment in the intercondylar notch region.
3.	Mucoid degeneration of anterior cruciate ligament.
4.	Ruptured Baker's cyst.
```
