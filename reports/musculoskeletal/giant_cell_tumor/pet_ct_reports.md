WHOLE BODY F18 FDG PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: Left gluteal /iliac bone giant cell tumor ; for staging evaluation.

Technique:

Whole body images (vertex to mid-thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. Blood sugar level was checked before the scan and was 96mg/dl.

Findings:

HEAD and NECK: 

No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in region of head and neck.
There is no significant cervical or supraclavicular adenopathy. The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.








CHEST:

Multiple intensely hypermetabolic bilateral pulmonary nodules, largest in left lung upper lobe measures 2.2 cm with SUVmax 13.0.
 No abnormalities are detected in breast, bilateral axilla or internal mammary node.
No abnormalities noted in the mediastinum and vasculature.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 

No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of abdomen and pelvis.
Ametabolic simple cortical cyst noted in right kidney, measuring 1 cm.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen, pancreas and adrenals are unremarkable. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.

SKELETON: 

Intensely hypermetabolic lytic lesion with soft tissue component noted involving left iliac bone and left ala of sacrum, infiltrating  left gluteus medius, Maximus and left paravertebral muscles ,measuring  7 x 5.6 cm ,with SUVmax of 18.3.
 






IMPRESSION:

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC LYTIC LESION WITH SOFT TISSUE COMPONENT INVOLVING LEFT ILIAC BONE AND LEFT ALA OF  SACRUM, INFILTRATING ADJACENT MUSCLES ; CONSISTENT WITH PRIMARY MALIGNANCY.

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC BILATERAL PULMONARY NODULES; CONSISTENT WITH METASTASIS.

NO DEFINITE EVIDENCE OF METABOLICALLY ACTIVE FDG AVID DISEASE ELSEWHERE IN THE WHOLE BODY SURVEY.
