```text
Thorax with Abdomen  (C.T. SCAN) of 26.07.2011:

C.T.SCAN OF THORAX

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

Diffuse centriacinar emphysematous changes  noted in both lungs  predominantly involving the upper lobes. Scattered areas of air space opacity throughout the showing cavitation noted in both upper lobes.  The areas of fibrosis is noted in the apical segment of both upper lobes.  Paraseptal emphysematous changes noted in the apical segment of the right upper lobe. 

Few lymph nodes noted in the pretracheal, bilateral paratracheal, and subcarinal region, the largest measuring 1 cm in short axis.  the largest one is noted in the subcarinal region

Diffuse atherosclerotic changes noted in the form of calcified and noncalcified plaque involving the aorta and iliac vessels.

There is no evidence of pleural effusion seen.

C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

Peripherally calcified hypodense lesions, three in number, two in the right and one on the left lobe of liver is noted. The largest measuring 3.9 X 3 cm in segment 7 of right lobe of liver. no evidence of enhancement noted with contrast. Another calcified focus noted in the subdiaphragmatic region on the right side.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well seen and are within normal limits.

The gall bladder, spleen and adrenals are normal.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of  pelvicaliceal system.

Multiple enlarged lymph nodes are varying size  noted in the  paraaortic, aortocaval, retrocaval regions. The largest measuring 9mm  in size.  Few  lymph nodes shows central non enhancing regions s/o necrosis.. Mild increase in the peri nodal density suggestive of fat stranding.  

There is no evidence of free fluid seen in the abdomen.

The bladder is normally seen.

Prostate appears normal.

The degenerative changes noted in the lumbar spine.  

IMPRESSION: 
1 Air space opacities, few of them showing cavitation involving the upper lobes of both lungs, with mediastinal and retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy as detailed .  The possibility of underlying infection (tuberculosis)  to be considered.
2.  Diffuse centriacinar  emphysematous changes in both lungs.
3..  Chronic healed abscesses in the liver as detailed.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `3079`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / Erect X-Ray Abdomen`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF THORAX WITH ABDOMEN

THORAX

Serial axial sections of the thorax were studied after administration of I.V.Bolus contrast.

Minimal pneumothorax noted on the left side.

Minimal bilateral pleural effusion noted.

The left intercostal drainage tube is noted in the lung parenchyma with adjacent air space opacities noted along the tract.

Rest of lung parenchyma is normal.

The study shows even distribution of the pulmonary arterial branches and that of the bronchial tree.

The anatomical configuration of the structures in the mediastinum and both hilar regions are normal.  

There is no evidence of hilar lymphadenopathy seen.

The arch of the aorta and the vessels arising from it are normal.

Fracture of anterior aspect of the 8th rib is noted on the right side.

ABDOMEN:
Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

Grade III liver laceration noted involving the segment five and six of the right lobe. No evidence of active contrast extravasation. Rest of liver parenchyma is normal. 

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well seen and are within normal limits.

The gall bladder, spleen and adrenals are normal.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of  pelvicaliceal system.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

Mild haemoperitoneum noted in the perihepatic region and in pelvis.

No evidence of pneumoperitoneum.

The bladder is normally seen.

Uterus and ovaries appears normal.

IMPRESSION: 

1.	Grade III liver laceration with mild haemoperitoneum.

2.	Left minimal pneumothorax with bilateral minimal pleural effusion.

3.	Fracture of the 8th rib on right side.
```
