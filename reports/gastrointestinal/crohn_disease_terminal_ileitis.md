The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well demonstrated and are normal in size, shape and attenuation.

The gall bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall.
No evidence of any stone, sludge or mass.

The spleen is normal with regard to size, position and morphology and displays uniform enhancement pattern.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of pelvicaliceal system.
The perinephric area is normal.

The adrenals are normal on both sides.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

Short segment bowel wall narrowing with wall thickening for a length of 2.5cm noted in the distal ileum with dilatation of proximal small bowel.

Mild free fluid noted in the pelvis and in interloop bowel region.
The bowel walls are enhancing normally.
Duodenum and jejunum are collapsed probably due to vomiting.

Multiple mesenteric nodes noted, largest measuring 9mm.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

IMPRESSION: 

Known c/o crohn's disease with ileal stricture.
1.	Short segment narrowing with thickening of distal ileum with dilatation of proximal small bowels - possibly suggestive of stricture.
2.	Minimal free fluid in pelvis and interloop region.

The perinephric area is normal.
The adrenals are normal on both sides.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

Long segment irregular circumferential wall thickening with intervening normal bowel loops involving the proximal ileal loops with increased vascularity noted.  The maximal wall thickness measures about 7.7mm.  Multiple lymph nodes noted in the mesentery, the largest one measuring 8.8mm in size.  No evidence of any calcification or necrosis noted within the lymph nodes.  Minimal haziness noted around the mesenteric vessels..  No evidence of arterial or venous thrombosis noted.

There is minimal free fluid seen in the pelvis.

The bladder is well distended and displays a normal wall. No evidence of stone or mass.

The uterus and ovaries appears  normal.

IMPRESSION: 

Long segment irregular circumferential wall thickening with intervening normal bowel loops involving proximal ileal loopsand mesenteric lymphadenopathy detailed - features suggestive of inflammatory bowel disease(CROHN'S DISEASE). 

Suggested further evaluation if indicated.
