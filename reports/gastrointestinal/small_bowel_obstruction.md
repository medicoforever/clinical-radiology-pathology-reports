### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1705`)
**Modality Context:** `CT Abdomen & Pelvis`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

A large well-defined mass lesion measuring 16.5 X 10.7 X 11.5 cm is noted predominantly involving segment IV of the left lobe of liver with a large exophytic component inferiorly. The lesion shows peripheral nodular enhancement on arterial phase with subsequent centripetal filling of venous and delayed phases.  Few specks of calcification noted within the lesion - s/o giant hepatic hemangioma. two small lesions showing similar enhancement characteristics is noted in segment 2 and 3 of the left lobe of liver.
Mild  intrahepatic biliary radicles dilatation noted in the right lobe of liver.  However, there CBD is normal in calibre.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well seen and are within normal limits.

The gall bladder, spleen and adrenals are normal.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of  pelvicaliceal system. Few tiny cortical cysts noted in both kidneys.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy noted.

Umbilical hernia noted with peritoneal defect measuring 1.6 X 1.5 cm with omental fat and small bowel as it is content. The small bowel loop in the hernia sac bowel wall thinning and poor wall enhancement. The proximal small bowel loops are dilated, measuring 3.5 cm in caliber - suggestive of obstruction. The distal small bowel loops and the large bowel loops are collapsed.

Mild ascites noted.

The bladder is normally seen.

Uterus and ovaries:  Normal.

Diffuse osteopenia  noted. sacralisation of  L5 vertebra noted with bilateral pars interarticularis defect at L4 level with L4-L5 grade II anterolisthesis. Wedge compression noted at D9 level.  Schmorl's node with mild wedging noted at the D12 level.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Obstructed umbilical hernia  with poor contrast enhancement and wall thinning of the intrahernial small bowel loop.
"	Mild ascites.
"	Hepatic haemangiomas with a giant hemangioma arising from the left lobe with mild intrahepatic biliary radical dilatation as detailed.
"	Diffuse osteopenia with sacralisation of  L5 vertebra and bilateral pars interarticularis defect at L4 level with L4-L5 grade II anterolisthesis. Wedge compression at D9 level.  Schmorl's node with mild wedging  at the D12 level.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1913`)
**Modality Context:** `CT Abdomen & Pelvis`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF ABDOMEN

Serial axial sections of the abdomen were studied with oral and I.V.Bolus contrast.

A large heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion measuring 9.2 X 12.0 X 9.6cms is noted predominantly in the left side of the pelvis. No evidence of calcification within. The left ovary is not seen separate from the mass lesion.
The lesion is abutting the distal sigmoid colon in its posteromedial aspect with focal loss of fat planes with it. It is also abutting the proximal sigmoid colon in a few sections.
Inferiorly the lesion is indenting the dome of the bladder with focal loss of intervening fat planes.
Laterally the lesion is abutting the left ureter at the level of S1 in a few sections; however there is no evidence of proximal hydroureteronephrosis to suggest obstruction.
Uterus and right ovary: Normal and is free from the lesion.

Normal anatomical configuration of the liver noted with uniform density.

The head, body and tail of the pancreas are well seen and are within normal limits.

The gall bladder, spleen and adrenals are normal.

Both the kidneys are normal in size, shape and position and show good excretion of contrast with adequate demonstration of  pelvicaliceal system.

Aorta, IVC and paraaortic areas are normal.  No para-aortic lymphadenopathy noted.

Rest of the bowel loops are normal.

The omentum is thickened with diffusely altered density suggestive of omental deposits.
Minimal ascites noted with enhancing peritoneum.

A small nodule is noted in the lateral basal segment of the right lower lobe.
Minimal right pleural effusion noted.

A sclerotic focus is noted in the right iliac bone suggestive of bony island.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Large heterogeneous enhancing adnexal mass lesion probably of left ovarian origin - malignant etiology.
"	Omental thickening and diffuse heterogenicity - s/o deposits.
"	Minimal ascites with peritoneal enchancement - possibility of peritoneal metastasis to be considered.
"	Small lung nodule with minimal right pleural effusion - needs further evaluation.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `2189`)
**Modality Context:** `CT Abdomen & Pelvis`  

```text
INTRAVENOUS UROGRAM

Patient a known case of chronic renal failure. Status post renal transplant.

Post operative changes noted in the right iliac region in the form of clips.

The renal and psoas outlines are well seen. The native kidneys appear small in size.

Normal gas pattern of the bowel seen.

Following injection of I.V. Iodinated contrast the film taken at 5 minutes shows prompt excretion of contrast by the transplant kidney.

Transplant kidney shows mild prominence of pelvicalyceal system. 

The bladder shows smooth contour.

The post void study shows complete emptying of the bladder.

IMPRESSION: 
Patient a known case of chronic renal failure. Status post renal transplant.
Present study shows mild prominence of pelvicalyceal system in the transplant kidney.
Normally excreting transplant kidney with no evidence of obstruction.

DR.DEYANAND.
```
