Technique: sag, cor, ax T2, ax T1, postcontrast dynamic T1, DWI, T1 vibe.

posterolaterally the lesion is seen to extend upto the mesorectal fascia  at 8 o'clock position.
 There is loss of fat plane with infiltration noted of the right lavetor ani muscle.
There is loss of fat plane between the lesion and the right coccygeus muscle.

Few liver cysts are noted which were also seen previously.
There is a CSF signal intensity lesion measuring 5.7 x 1.2 x 1.9 cm (craniocaudal by transverse by anteroposterior) noted in the sacral canal extending from S2 to S4 level, displacing the sacral nerve roots with scalloping of the bony wall. This may represent occult sacral meningocele.
Compared to previous scan dated 21 October 2010 present scan shows reduction in the craniocaudal extent of the rectal mass lesion. No other significant interval change in size and extent of the lesion.
No significant interval change in the number and size of the lymph nodes.
