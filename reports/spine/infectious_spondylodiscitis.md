### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `5820`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Spine + Contrast`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF LUMBOSACRAL SPINE- 12.9.11

Technique: Sagittal, T1, T2, IR, Axial T2, IR Coronal for SI joints.
.                  Post contrast T1 sagittal, axial, coronal.

There is destruction of L4 vertebral body predominantly involving the inferior half of the vertebral body with evidence of mild kyphosis at L4-L5 level.

Disc extrusion with loss of intervertebral disc height at L4-L5 level in both anterior and posterior aspect.  Secondary spinal canal stenosis is noted measuring 2.5mm in maximum diameter causing compression of cauda equine fibers.

Destruction of anterosuperior margin of L5 vertebra is also seen.  Posterior elements are normal in both L4 and L5 levels.

Altered bone marrow signal is noted in L4 and L5 vertebral body sparing the posterior aspect of L5 vertebral body which is seen as heterogenous signal in T2, hypointense on T1 and hyperintense on IR.  On post contrast there is enhancement with bilateral paravertebral and prevertebral enhancing soft tissue measuring 6.5mm in bilateral paravertebral location and 1.0cm in prevertebral location.

No evidence of restricted diffusion seen.

No evidence of extradural enhancing soft tissue noted.

Mild diffuse annular disc bulge is seen at L3-L4 level with no significant interval changes.

Rest of the disc in lumbosacral spine is normal.

Limited C.T.correlation  shows multiple calcific foci in the spleen.
Multiple calcified lymphnodes are seen in periportal and peripancreatic loction in the region of head of pancreas.

IMPRESSION: 

Features suggestive of spondylodiscitis with destruction of vertebrae at L4-L5 level as detailed with adjacent soft tissue causing secondary canal stenosis. 
To rule out pott's spine.
```
