### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `10714`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / X-ray Spine & SI Joints`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF CERVICAL SPINE

Technique: Sagittal T1,T2 and IR, Axial T2.

The cervical spine displays a normal lordotic curvature and vertebral column alignment.

The individual vertebrae are normal in heights and morphology.

Focal area of increased signal intensity is noted involving the corners of the vertebral body.

Focal area of increased in signal intensity is noted in C3-C4, C5-C6 and C6-C7 intervertebral disc.

There is no evidence of any significant disc bulge or prolapse.

Linear hyperintensity is noted involving the right half of cervical spinal cord at C5 and C6 level.However no associated cord swelling or atrophy.

The prevertebral and paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

The cranio vertebral junction and atlanto axial joint are normal.

Screening of dorsal spine:

Decrease in intervertebral disc space height with focal increase in signal intensity involving the D6-7 to D12-L1 intervertebral disc.

Hyperintensity involving the corners and endplates of the dorsal vertebra predominately in the lower level.

Areas of sclerosis involving the anterior corners of D11 and D12 vertebral body noted.
Small haemangioma is noted involving the D10 vertebral body.

Screening of the lumbar spine:

Increase in signal intensity involving the endplates and corners of the lumbar vertebra.
Decrease in intervertebral disc space is noted from L1-2 to L3-4 level.

Hyperintensity is noted involving the all lumbar intervertebral disc except at L4-5 level.

Screening of the CT spine shows ankylosis of the vertebral body, costotransverse, costovertebral joints, facet joints and sacroiliac joints.

IMPRESSION: 

"	The imaging features are suggestive of ANKYLOSING SPONDYLITIS INVOLVING THE SPINE with degenerative changes involving the disc.
"	Linear hyperintensity involving the right half of cervical spinal cord at C5 and C6 level. However no associated cord swelling or atrophy.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `21238`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / X-ray Spine & SI Joints`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF  WHOLE SPINE

Patient a known case of ankylosing spondylitis.
Diffuse osteopenia the visualised bones noted.
Ankylosis of the bilateral sacroiliac joint, facet joint, costotransverse and costovertebral joints are noted.
Ossification of the interspinous and the anterior longitudinal ligament is also noted.
Transverse displaced fracture involving the mid vertebral body of C7 vertebra with the fracture line extension into the right pedicle and bilateral superior articular process of C7 vertebra noted.  Grade I anterior subluxation of the proximal vertebral segment noted.
However no evidence of significant spinal canal compromise.
Undisplaced transverse fracture is noted involving the mid L5 vertebral body with fracture line extension into the bilateral pedicles, right superior articular process of L5 vertebra and spinous process of the L4 lumbar vertebra.
No other fracture is noted in the visualised bones.
Orthopaedic implants are noted involving both hip joint.
Mild anterior subluxation of the bilateral temporomandibular joint is noted.
IMPRESSION:
Patient a known case of ankylosing spondylitis. Present scan shows 
1)	Transverse displaced fracture involving the C7 vertebra with grade I anterior subluxation of the proximal vertebral segment as detailed. However no significant spinal canal compromise.
2)	Undisplaced fracture of involving the L5 vertebra with extension as detailed.
3)	Diffuse osteopenia.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `21446`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / X-ray Spine & SI Joints`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF CERVICAL SPINE

Patient a known case of ankylosing spondylitis with transverse displaced fracture involving the C7 vertebra with grade I anterior subluxation of the proximal vertebral segment  and undisplaced fracture of L5 vertebra for evaluation of the cord.

Technique: Sagittal T1, STIR and T2, Axial T2, T2 and STIR Sagittal - rest of the spine.

There is an extradural hematoma in the posterior aspect of the canal extending from C2 to midbody of D3 vertebra. The hematoma is hypointense on T2, iso to minimally hyperintense on T1 and heterogeneously hypointense on STIR suggestive of hematoma in acute stage. The hematoma measures 14 x 0.8 x 1.7 cm (CCX AP X TR). The hematoma is seen to compressand displace the cord anteriorly. Posterior spinal muscles shows hyperintense signal suggestive of contusion / edema.

Small anterior subligamentous extradural hematoma with similar signal intensity is also seen at C6 vertebral level measuring 1.6 x 0.3 cm (CC X AP)

Hemangioma is seen in D4 vertebral body.

Heterogenously hyperintense signal is noted in L5 vertebra.

Rest of the vertebra shows normal marrow signals.

IMPRESSION: 

Patient a known case of ankylosing spondylitis with transverse displaced fracture involving the C7 vertebra with grade I anterior subluxation of the proximal vertebral segment  and undisplaced fracture of L5 vertebra for evaluation of the cord. Present study shows,
1.	Acute hematoma in the posterior aspect of the canal extending from C2 to midbody of D3 vertebra with cord compression and anterior displacement.
2.	Small anterior extradural acute hematoma at C6 level.
```
