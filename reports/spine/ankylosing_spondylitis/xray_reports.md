```text
MRI SCAN OF LUMBOSACRAL SPINE

Technique: Sagittal, T1, T2, IR, Axial T2, IR Coronal for SI joints

The lumbosacral spine displays a normal lordotic curvature and vertebral column alignment.

The individual vertebrae are normal in heights, morphology and bone marrow signal intensities.

Mild disc desiccation is seen at the L4-5 level. Rest of the intervertebral discs is normal in heights and hydration. The vertebral end-plates are normal.

L4-5: Diffuse annular disc bulge with a small focal left foraminal protrusion is seen indenting the exiting L4 nerve root.

L5-S1: Loss of posterior concavity is seen.

L1-2, L2-3, L3-4 intervertebral disks appear normal.  

Facetal arthropathy changes are seen at L4-5 and L5-S1 levels. Tiny synovial cyst is seen at the left L5- S1 facet joint.  

The conus medullaris ends at L1 level and the cauda equine is normal in appearance.

The prevertebral and paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

SI joints: 
Sclerosis, erosions and edematous changes are seen in the subarticular region of the sacrum and iliac bones on both sides.
Small subarticular cyst is seen in the left iliac bone.  No abnormal collection/adjacent soft tissue changes seen in the present study.  

Screening of the cervical spine:  Mild disc osteophyte complex causing mild indentation of the anterior thecal sac at C4-5, C5-6, C6-7 and C7-T1 levels.

Screening of dorsal spine:  Mild diffuse disc bulge is seen at the D3-4, D4-5, and D6-7 levels. 

IMPRESSION:

Bilateral sacroiliitis.

Diffuse annular disc bulge with focal left foraminal protrusion indenting the exiting L4 nerve root at L4-5 level.
```
