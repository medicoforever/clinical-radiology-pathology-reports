WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN

Clinical Indication: - D5 vertebral lesion ? primary; for evaluation.

Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid-thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. Blood sugar level was checked before the scan and was 151mg/dl.

Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
Mild asymmetric prominence of right tonsil. No abnormal enhancement seen.
Ametabolic bilateral level IA, IB, II lymph nodes seen largest measuring 7.6mm insignificant by size criteria.
Mucosal thickening causing complete opacification of right frontal and right ethmoidal sinuses consistent with sinusitis.
The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.

CHEST:
Small mildly hypermetabolic right paratracheal, pretracheal and subcarinal lymph nodes seen largest 7mm in size with an SUV max of 2.2.
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
Minimal tubular bronchiectasis seen in posterior segment of right upper lobe with associated fibrosis and minimal pleural thickening seen along the posteromedial chest wall. 
Ametabolic single small nodule measuring 2.8mm in size seen in apical segment of right upper lobe indeterminate likely granuloma.
Small ametabolic bilateral axillary lymph nodes largest 6mm on left side insignificant in size.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
Intensely hypermetabolic bilateral adrenal nodules seen measuring 1.8x1.4cm on the right (SUV max 6.4) and measuring 1.8x1.3cm on the left side arising from the lateral limb with an SUV max of 8.4.
Small ametabolic left paraaortic aortocaval and right ileocolic lymph nodes seen largest 6mm insignificant in size.
Non necrotic/non calcific peripancreatic and periportal lymph nodes largest 7mm.
Bilateral inguinal hernia seen. 
Two small ametabolic foci of benign calcification largest measuring 7mm seen in right iliac fossa underneath the parietal wall 
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas is unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.

SKELETAL: 
Intensely hypermetabolic lytic lesion seen in posteroinferior aspect of D5 vertebral body on the right side with destruction of the right lateral cortex with an SUV max of 22.9. There is associated right paravertebral soft tissue measuring 10mm causing right neural foraminal narrowing with a possible epidural component. No obvious cord compression seen.
Small ametabolic 8mm lytic lesion seen in S1 vertebral body on the right side.
Well defined ametabolic sclerotic lesion seen involving the right pedicle of D4 vertebral body.
Tiny ametabolic focal well defined sclerotic lesion in posterior aspect of left ilium.
Diffuse degenerative changes of spine are seen. Bilateral lysis of pars interarticularis at L5-S1 level. Bilateral osteoarthritic changes seen in knee joints.
Well defined ametabolic cystic lesion in bilateral popliteal fossa measuring 13mm on right and 9mm on the left side seen along the lower semimembranosus tendon consistent with ganglion cyst.

IMPRESSION:

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC LYTIC LESION INVOLVING D5 VERTEBRAL BODY WITH ASSOCIATED RIGHT PARAVERTEBRAL SOFT TISSUE AND RIGHT NEURAL FORAMINAL NARROWING; CONSISTENT WITH SKELETAL METASTASES.

INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC BILATERAL ADRENAL NODULES ; CONSISTENT WITH METASTASIS.

FOCAL INTRAMEDULLARY LYTIC LESION IN S1 VERTEBRAL BODY AND SCLEROTIC LESION IN D5 PEDICLE.

MILDLY HYPERMETABOLIC MEDIASTINAL LYMPHNODES.

OCCULT LUNG PRIMARY WITH ADRENAL AND BONE METASTASES. SUGGEST HPE CORRELATION FROM D5 VERTEBRA.
