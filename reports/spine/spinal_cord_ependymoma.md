### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `70534`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Spine`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF LUMBOSACRAL SPINE AND SCREENING OF REST OF THE SPINE

Technique: Sagittal, T1, T2, IR, Axial T2, IR Coronal for SI joints, T1 contrast 
	       Screening of rest of the spine - T2 Sagittal

The lumbosacral spine displays a normal lordotic curvature and vertebral column alignment.

The individual vertebrae are normal in heights, morphology and bone marrow signal intensities.

Disc desiccation is seen at the L3-4 and L4-5 levels.  The intervertebral discs are normal in heights.

The vertebral end-plates are normal.

L1-2, L2-3: Normal.

L3-4: Mild loss of posterior concavity is seen.

L4-5: Mild diffuse disc bulge causing mild anterior thecal sac indentation and indentation over right traversing L5 nerve root.

L5-S1: Normal.

Spinal canal region: 

A well-defined oval intensely enhancing lesion measuring approximately 2.25 X 1.51 X 1.36 cm (CC X TRAX AP) is seen at L4 vertebral level. The lesion is in intradural extramedullary position causing compression and displacement of the cauda equina at this level.

The conus medullaris ends at L1 level and the cauda equine is normal in appearance except the mass effect by the mass lesion.

Another rim enhancing dumbbell shaped lesion measuring approximately 1.86 X 1.41 X 1.38 cm (TRAX AP X CC) is seen at S2 level extending from the canal to the spinal foramen region. The left S2 foramen is expanded.  These lesions appear hypointense in T1 and heterogeneously hyper intense in T2 images. 

The prevertebral and paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

Both SI joints are normal in joint space and signal intensities.

Screening of cervical spine: Disc osteophyte complex causing mild anterior thecal sac indentation at C3-4, C4-5 and C5-6 levels.  No other significant abnormalities.

Screening of dorsal spine:  Vertebral body hemangioma is seen at D10 vertebral level.

Uterus:  A fibroid measuring approximately 1.6 X 1.2 cm is seen in the right lateral wall.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Well-defined oval intensely enhancing intradural extramedullary lesion at L4 vertebral level causing compression and displacement of the cauda equina as detailed. May represent nerve sheath tumor/ ependymoma.

"	Rim enhancing dumbbell shaped cystic lesion at S2 level as detailed, may represent cystic nerve sheath tumor.
"	Diffuse disc bulge causing anterior thecal sac indentation and indentation over right traversing L5 nerve root at L4-5 level.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `89626`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Spine`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN AND SPINE

Technique: MPR T1, FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, DWI, SWI, post contrast T1fs axial and sagittal for brain; T1 and T2 sagittal, post contrast T1 axial and sagittal for spine. 

A large lobulated mass lesion measuring approximately 5.1 x 4.7 x 4.3 cm in seen in the fourth ventricle, probably arising from the floor of the fourth ventricle.  The mass appears mildly hyperintense to brain parenchyma on T2-weighted imaging and isointense on T1-weighted imaging.  Few areas of T2 hyperintensity are noted within the mass suggesting cystic/ necrotic changes.  Multiple vessels are also seen coursing through the mass. No significant diffusion restriction is noted.  No significant blooming is noted on SWI. On post contrast imaging, the mass shows heterogeneous enhancement with peripheral enhancement around the cystic/necrotic regions and minimal to no enhancement in the solid areas. 

Superiorly, the mass is limited to the fourth ventricle.  On the left side, the mass is seen to extend into the foramina of Lushka, and thence into the left cerebello pontine cistern compressing and displacing the brain stem to the right.  On the right side, mild extension into the foramen of Lushka is noted. Inferiorly, the mass is seen to extend into the spinal canal upto the level of C3-C4 intervertebral disc and is seen to encase the spinal cord anteriorly, posteriorly and in the left lateral aspect.  The spinal cord is compressed and displaced to the right. Anteriorly, the mass is seen to extend into the prepontine cistern encasing the basilar artery and its branches.  However, the normal flow void and caliber of the basilar artery is maintained. Posteriorly, the mass is seen to extend to the cisterna magna. The cerebellar vermis is displaced posteriorly and the cerebellar hemispheres laterally.

There is marked dilatation of the third ventricle and both lateral ventricles due to obstructive hydrocephalous with extensive transependymal CSF flow.

The supratentorial neurparenchyma appears normal.

No abnormal meningeal/ ependymal enhancement noted. 

The spinal cord is normal in dimensions and signal intensity throughout its length.  There is no abnormal signal intensity / enhancing mass noted within the subarachnoid space in the entire length of the spinal canal. The vertebrae and intervertebral disks appear normal.

IMPRESSION:

"	Large heterogeneously enhancing fourth ventricular mass lesion extending into foramina of the ventricle and cervical spinal canal as detailed. Imaging features favor the diagnosis of ependymoma. 
"	No definite spinal metastases were detected.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `97522`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Spine`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF CERVICAL SPINE AND SCREENING OF REST OF THE SPINE

Technique: Sagittal T1, IR and T2, Axial T2.
	        Screening of rest of the spine T2 sagittal.
                    Post Contrast Images - FS T1 in all three planes of cervical spine. Post contrast FS 
                                                         T1 for rest of spine and 3D T1 MPRAGE for brain. 
                   

The cervical spine displays a loss of lordotic curvature and normal vertebral column alignment.

The individual vertebrae are normal in heights, morphology and bone marrow signal intensities.

C3-C4, C4-C5 , C5-C6 - small central disc protrusion indenting thecal sac noted.

C6- C7:  Diffuse disc bulge indenting thecal sac noted

2.4 x1x1.3 cm (CC x AP x TR) sized intra medullary focal lesion appearing isointense on T1WI and hyperintense on T2WI at C3, C4 level. Mild expansion of the cord is noted at this level. Extensive hyperintensity is noted within the cord from cervico medullay junction till D2 level - may represent cord edema. With contrast, moderate homogenous enhancement of the lesion is noted. Minimal linear extension of the enhancement is noted both cranially and caudally for a length of about 1cm. Rest of the areas with abnormal signal intensity within the cord shows no obvious enhancement. 

No other obvious abnormal enhancing focal lesions in rest of the spine and in the brain. 

The prevertebral and paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

The cranio vertebral junction and atlanto axial joint are normal.

Screening of rest of the spine and brain: No significant abnormalities noted.

IMPRESSION: 

Enhancing intra medullary focal lesion in the cervical spinal cord at C3,C4 level with cord edema as detailed - possibilities are 

A.	Intra medullary metastasis.
B.	Primary spinal cord tumor like ependymoma.
```
