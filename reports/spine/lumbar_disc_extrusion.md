```text
MRI SCAN OF LUMBOSACRAL SPINE

Technique: Sagittal, T1, T2, IR, Axial T2, IR Coronal for SI joints

Loss of lumbar lordosis noted.

Endplate changes noted at L4-L5  vertebral level.

Vertebral body alignment and signal intensity are normal.

L1 - L2 - normal
L2 - L3 - normal
L3 - L4 - diffuse annular disc bulge indenting the right exiting nerve root. 
L4 - L5 - diffuse annular disc bulge with a broad-based central extrusion and inferior migration  indenting bilateral exiting nerve root and compressing the thecal sac , with right facetal  joint arthropathy and  right ligamentum flavum hypertrophy,
L5 - S1 - diffuse annular disc bulge with central extrusion and inferior migration indenting the right traversing and left exiting nerve root.

Intervertebral discs show normal height and signal intensity.

There is no evidence of any disc bulge or disc protrusion.

Theca and intrathecal contents are normal.

Facet joints are normal.

Paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

Both sacro iliac joints show normal joint space and articular margins.

Screening of cervical spine;
Reversal of cervical lordosis.  Diffuse degenerative changes noted in the cervical spine.
Screening of the dorsal spine;
No significant abnormalities detected.

IMPRESSION:
1.Diffuse annular disc bulge indenting the right exiting nerve root at L3-L4 level.

2.Diffuse annular disc bulge with a broad-based central extrusion and inferior migration  indenting bilateral exiting nerve root and compressing the thecal sac , with right facetal  joint arthropathy and  right ligamentum flavum hypertrophy at L4 - L5 level.

3.Diffuse annular disc bulge with central extrusion and inferior migration indenting the right traversing and left exiting nerve root at L5 - S1 level.
```
