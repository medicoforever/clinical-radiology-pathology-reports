### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1672`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Cervical Spine`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF CERVICAL SPINE

MRI scan of cervical spine performed.

Technique: Sagittal, axial T1 and T2.

Mild reversal of cervical lordosis noted from C3-C6 level.

Vertebral body alignment and signal intensity are normal.

Disc desiccation changes noted at multiple levels.

C1-C2: Normal.

C2:C3: Normal.

C3-C4: DADB with bilateral uncovertebral joint arthropathy indenting the thecal sac and the left C4 nerve root.

C4-C5: The DADB with central and right paracentral disc protrusion with the right uncovertebral joint hypertrophy indenting the thecal sac and right exiting nerve root.

C5-C6:  DADB  with right paracentral broad based disc extrusion with inferior migration compressing the right hemicord and right exiting nerve root.  Left subarticular and foraminal disc protrusion indenting the left C6 nerve root. 
Mild hyperintensity on T2-weighted images noted within the  right hemicord at this level- may represent myelomalacic changes with secondary spinal canal stenosis measuring 6.3 mm in maximum dimension.

C6-C7:  Bilateral uncovertebral joint hypertrophy noted indenting the right C7 nerve root.

Cranio vertebral junction is normal.

Paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

IMPRESSION: 

DADB with broad based right paracentral disc extrusion  at C5-C6 level causing secondary canal stenosis, compressing the right hemicord and right exiting nerve root at that level.  Left subarticular and foraminal disc protrusion indenting the left C6 nerve root. 

Myelomalacia changes at C5-C6 level.

Multilevel disc disease as detailed.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `3420`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Cervical Spine`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF CERVICAL SPINE WITH SCREENING OF WHOLE SPINE

Technique: Sagittal T1, T2 & IR, Axial T2.

There is loss of cervical lordosis. 

Evidence of post - op changes with partial fusion in C3-C4 vertebral bodies. 

There is reduction in height of the C5 and C6 vertebral bodies. 
Degenerative changes in the form anterior, posterior and lateral osteophytes seen involving all the cervical vertebrae.

Diffuse annular disc bulge at C2-C3 level causing no significant mass effect.

Bilateral uncoverterbal joint degeneration and posterior disc osteophyte complex causing spinal canal stenosis is seen at C3-C4 to C6-C7 levels with cord compression, and bilateral neural foraminal narrowing and indenting exiting nerve roots. The cord at C3-C4 level is thinned out and shows hyperintensity along right lateral aspect suggestive of edema / myelomalacia. 
 
Cranio vertebral junction is normal.

Screening of dorsal spine shows small disc bulges in mid dorsal level, causing no significant mass effect. 

Screening of lumbar spine shows diffuse annular disc bulges at L1-L2 to L5-S1 levels. This needs full study for further evaluation. 

IMPRESSION: Severe degenerative changes in cervical spine causing secondary canal stenosis and mass effect over exiting nerve roots as detailed. 
                            Cord edema / myelomalacia at C3-C4 level.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `5034`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Cervical Spine`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF CERVICAL SPINE WITH SCREENING OF WHOLE SPINE

Technique: T1 T2 sagittal, GRE T2 axial, Screening T2 sagittal.

The cervical spine displays loss of cervical lordosis.
 
The individual vertebrae are normal in heights, morphology and bone marrow signal intensities.

The intervertebral discs are normal in heights and hydration.
The vertebral end-plates are normal.

C2-C3 disc shows diffuse disc bulge indenting thecal sac.

C3-C4 disc shows diffuse disc bulge with large central disc protrusion causing moderate canal stenosis with indentation over the cord.

C4-C5 disc shows diffuse disc bulge with central and left centrolateral disc protrusion indenting the cord.

C5-C6 disc shows diffuse disc bulge indenting the cord.

C6-C7 level shows diffuse disc bulge with right centrolateral disc protrusion indenting the cord with narrowing of right neural foramen.

C7-D1 disc is normal.

Hyperintense signal intensity noted involving the cord at C3 level suggestive of cord oedema / myelomalacia.

The prevertebral and paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

The cranio vertebral junction and atlanto axial joint are normal.

Screening study of dorsal spine revealed mild anterior wedge compression involving D11 and D12 vertebral bodies.

Screening study of lumbar spine revealed disc desiccation with diffuse disc bulge indenting thecal sac at L3-L4 to L5-S1 levels more severe at L4-L5 level.  Needs detailed study.

IMPRESSION:

1.	Multi level disc bulges and protrusions in cervical spine as detailed.
2.	Hyperintense signal intensity noted involving the cord at C3 level suggestive of cord oedema / myelomalacia.
Suggested : Detailed study of lumbar spine if indicated.
```
