### Case #01 — BEFUND_UBEID: `371`
**Imaging Modality Context:** `MRI DWI/ADC, MR Angiography, Non-Contrast CT Brain, CT Angiography`  

```text
BRAIN MRI REPORT - 25.07.2011:
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: FLAIR Ax, T1 Sag, T2 Cor, DWI, SWI.

There is prominence of the cortical sulci and bilateral sylvian fissures suggestive of age-related cerebral atrophy. The ventricular system also appears prominent. 
Hyperintense signal is noted in the periventricular and subcortical white matter on FLAIR images, suggestive of chronic ischaemic changes. 

There is a small focus of hyperintensity noted in the left middle cerebellar peduncle on FLAIR images, which shows restricted diffusion on DWI sequences. There is no evidence of intracranial bleed seen.

Few old lacunar infarcts are seen scattered in the left frontal lobe, pons and the left cerebellum.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.

IMPRESSION: Age-related cerebral atrophy with white matter ischaemic changes and lacunar infarcts.
Acute infarct in left middle cerebellar peduncle.

         						DR.MANISH
```
