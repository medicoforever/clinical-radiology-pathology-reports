### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1747`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Brain + Contrast`  

```text
MRI BRAIN AND ORBIT

Technique: FLAIR axial, T1, IR coronal,T1axial FS,POST CONTRAST T1 axial,T1 cor, T1 MPR.

Soft tissue swelling  in the extra conal plane noted adjacent to the insertion site  of the lateral rectus of the left eye which appears hypointense on T1, hyperintense on T2 and IR sequences.  Preseptal soft tissue thickening noted on the left side.
The lesion shows homogeneous enhancement on contrast except for a central nonenhancing area measuring 0.8 X 0.4 X 1.0 cm suggestive of abscess. Adjacent lateral rectus muscle is normal in size and shows normal intensity.The soft tissue swelling is indenting the lateral wall of the globe. No evidence of cystic  or  calcified lesion in the left orbit.Intra conal  fat appear normal.  Extraocular muscles appear normal.  Rest of the left orbit appears normal.  Adjacent bone shows no  altered signal intensity.  No evidence of bony erosion.  Left optic nerve is normal.
Right orbit and its contents normal.
Thickening of soft tissue noted adjacent to the left zygomatic arch.
Neuro parenchyma is normal.  No evidence of focal enhancing lesion noted.
Impression:
Features suggestive of left orbital cellulitis with abscess formation.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1886`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Brain + Contrast`  

```text
MRI BRAIN AND ORBITS
Technique:  FLAIR, T2 axial, IR coronal, postcontrast T1 coronal, axial, sagittal.
The lateral rectus muscle of the left orbit is enlarged and thickened with heterogeneous area which shows peripheral hyperintense rim and central hypointensity on T1-weighted images and peripheral hypointense rim with central hyperintensity on T2-weighted images and IR sequences.
Anteriorly the lesion extends into preseptal space and posteriorly extends up to the apex of the orbits, optic canal and superior orbital fissure.  
The lesion indents the superolateral aspect of the left globe and medially it extremely displaces and compresses the optic nerve sheath.  
On postcontrast, the muscle shows enhancement with a central nonenhancing area measuring 2.9 X 0.9 X 0.9 cm (AP X T X CC) suggestive of abscess.
There is also abnormal dural enhancement along the left anterior cavernous region and along the left medial temporal lobe.
Neuroparenchyma is normal.
Impression:
Imaging features are consistent with myositis with abscess involving lateral rectus of the left orbit with extensions  and mass effect as detailed.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `5838`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Brain + Contrast`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique:  FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, t1 3D, DWI, SWI.
.                   Post contrast T1 3D.

A lesion noted in the left cerebellar hemisphere measuring 3.5 x 3.6 x 3.6cm (AP x Tr x CC) which shows hyperintense center and hypointense rim on T2 WI and in T1 WI , it shows hypointense  center and iso to hyperintense rim compared to gray matter.

Hyperintensity noted and the lesion in T2 WI suggestive of oedema with mass effect over the 4th ventricle and displacement of the brain stem to opposite side causing early obstructive hydrocephalus.  The lesion does not show restricted diffusion, however an irregular rim enhancement on post contrast images noted.

No abnormal enhancement of meniges noted.

Spectroscopy shows no abnormal elevation of choline noted even in the enhancing portion.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.

IMPRESSION: 

Intraaxial lesion with irregular rim enhancement and mass effect in left cerebellar region as detailed.  Inview of acute clinical presentation, hypointense rim with hyperintense centre on T2 WI and no significant choline peak in spectroscopy, possibility of cerebellar abscess to be considered.
SUGGESTED: HPE correlation.
```
