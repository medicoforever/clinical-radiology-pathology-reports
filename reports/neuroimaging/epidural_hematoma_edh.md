**Modality Context:** `CT Brain`  

```text
MRI BRAIN WITH MRA  (M.R.I. SCAN) of 13.09.2011:

Technique:   T1 mprage, FLAIR axial,  T2  Coronal, diffusion studies with MR angiography.

The brain  parenchyma  is normal in signal intensity and morphology.  There is evidence of age-related cerebral atrophy.  The ventricles are mildly prominent consistent with age-related cerebral atrophy.
Post  fossa  structures and the  brainstem are normal.     
No focal  lesions are seen. 
No evidence of any intra-axial or extra-axial bleed or hematoma.
The sella and parta-sellar areas are normal.
The right vertebral artery is hypoplastic. The rest of the vessels are nor remarkable for any abnormality.
The cranial vault is normal.
The CV junctions is also normal.
Screening of the cervical spine:  There is a 9 mm thick posterior epidural collection which is isointense to cord on T1 and hyperintense on T2W images. The cord is displaced anteriorly and compressed. It extends from C5/C6 level till D8/9 level.
The dorsal spine is otherwise normal.
There is a grade 1 spondylolisthesis of L5 over S1 with a widened spinal canal consistent with bilateral pars interarticularis defects.
IMPRESSION:
1.	Normal  study of the brain
2.	Posterior epidural collection from C5/6 till D8/9 causing anterior displacement of the cord.
3.	Grade 1 spondylolisthesis of L5 over S1 with pars interarticularis defects.
```
