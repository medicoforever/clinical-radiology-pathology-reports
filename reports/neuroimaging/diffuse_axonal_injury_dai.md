### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `6594`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI SWI / CT Brain`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF BRAIN (PLAIN)

Follow up C.T.Scan of the brain were studied from the base to the vertex.

Patient a case of RTA with features of diffuse axonal injury involving bilateral frontal, bilateral basifrontal, bilateral lentiform and bilateral temporal lobes.

As compared to the previous CT done on  12.09.11, present CT shows loss of gray white matter differentiation involving bilateral cerebral and cerebellar hemispheres.

Hypodensities noted in bilateral caudate, lentiform nucleus, bilateral basifrontal and bifrontal parasagittal region and bilateral high parietal regions and periaqueductal region.

Illdefined hyperdense areas noted in right high parietal region suggestive of haemorrhagic foci.

IMPRESSION:

 Known c/o RTA with features of hypodensities in bilateral cerebral hemispheres as detailed with loss of gray white matter differentiation.  Suggested MRI for further evaluation to rule out ischemia.  
Hyperdense areas in right parietal region suggestive of haemorrhage.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `7980`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI SWI / CT Brain`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF CERVICAL SPINE WITH BRAIN SCREENING

Known case of RTA, with cervical spine fracture (C4 and C5)

Technique: Sagittal T1, STIR and T2, Axial T2.
                    Brain: Axial FLAIR, DWI, SWI.     

There is fracture of C4 and C5 vertebral bodies which shows hyperintense signal. The C4-5 disc also shows hyperintense signal. The fracture fragment at C5 vertebra on the right side is displaced posteriorly causing secondary canal narrowing and cord compression and mild displacement of the cord to the left side. The anterior and posterior longitudinal ligaments and ligamentum flavum are intact at this level.

The cord at the level of C4 and C5 vertebral levels shows hyperintense signal suggestive of cord contusion / edema.

The prevertebral and paravertebral soft tissues show hyperintense signals suggestive of contusion.

The cranio vertebral junction and atlanto axial joint are normal.

The left vertebral artery shows hyperintense signal with absence of flow void suggestive of dissection probably secondary to dissection.

Screening of the rest of the spine shows hyperintense signal in D2-4 vertebral bodies suggestive of impaction fractures. Wedge compression fracture of D5, 6, 9 vertebrae.

Screening of the brain shows hemorrhagic contusions in the right thalamus and medial temporal lobe and frontal region. Areas of blooming are seen in bilateral frontotemporoparietal region, Splenium of corpus callosum and left lentiform nucleus and posterior midbrain.
Areas of diffusion restriction are seen in bilateral cerebellar hemispheres.

IMPRESSION: 

1.	Fracture involving the vertebral bodies of C4 and C5 with posterior displacement of fracture fragment at C5 vertebral body causing cord compression and signal change at this level.
2.	Impaction and wedge compression fractures of dorsal vertebrae as detailed.
3.	Screening of brain show features suggestive of diffuse axonal injury.
4.	Bilateral cerebellar acute infarcts with left vertebral artery occlusion probably secondary to dissection.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `8565`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI SWI / CT Brain`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF BRAIN (PLAIN)

Serial axial sections of the brain were studied from the base to the vertex.

Few haemorrhagic foci noted in bilateral frontal, bilateral temporal and right parietal lobes and bilateral cerebral peduncle suggestive of diffuse axonal injury.

Traumatic SAH noted in bilateral cerebral hemisphere and interpeduncular cistern.

Thin extraaxial hemorrhage measuring 3.0mm in thickness noted along the depressed fracture in left parietal bone.

The C.P.Angles are normal.

There is no midline shift seen.

The sella, parasellar and suprasellar regions are normal.

Subgaleal haematoma is noted along the left parietal convexity.

Comminuted depressed fracture of the left parietal bone noted, depressed for a depth of 9mm.

Hyperdensity noted over the depressed fractured fragment suggestive of foreign body.

Visualized part of both lung apices show air space opacities.

Cervical spine shows no evidence of fracture.

IMPRESSION: 

1.	Diffuse axonal injury.
2.	Thin left parietal extraaxial hemorrhage.
3.	Traumatic SAH.
4.	Fractures as detailed.

DR ASHITA/ DR MANISH
```
