### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1893`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / CT Brain`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF BRAIN (PLAIN AND CONTRAST)

Serial axial sections of the brain were studied from the base to the vertex.

A calcified extra-axial lesion showing no significant post contrast enhancement measuring 0.6 X 1.2 cm is noted along the left parietal convexity with no perilesional oedema suggestive of a calcified meningioma.

The sections of the brain do not reveal any area of altered tissue density.

The C.P.Angles and posterior fossa contents are normal.

The ventricular system shows normal anatomical configuration.

There is no midline shift seen.

The basal cisterns, cortical sulci and sylvian fissures are normal.

The sella, parasellar and suprasellar regions are normal.

Mucosal thickening noted in bilateral ethmoid and left frontal sinuses suggestive of sinusitis.

IMPRESSION: 

"	Features are suggestive of a small calcified left parietal meningioma as detailed.
```

Theca and intrathecal contents are normal.

Facet joints are normal.
Paravertebral soft tissues are normal.

Screening of whole spine shows posterior disc osteophyte complexes at C3-C4 to C5-C6 levels causing no significant mass effect. Mild diffuse annular disc bulge seen at L4-L5 and L5-S1 levels causing no significant mass effect.

IMPRESSION: Features are suggestive of Fatty hemangioma at D8 vertebral body level.
