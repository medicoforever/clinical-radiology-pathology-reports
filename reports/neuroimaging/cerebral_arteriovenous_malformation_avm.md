```text
BRAIN MRI REPORT - 27.07.2011:
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: T1, FLAIR axial, T2 coronal, DWI,SWI, TOF MRA neck and brain.

The area of hyperintensity noted in the left frontal lobe along with peripheral rim of hypointensity on T2-weighted images which shows blooming on susceptibility weighted images suggestive of chronic haemorrhage. No obvious flow voids noted within. MRA did not show any abnormal vessels to suggest any residual arteriovenous malformation.

Area of small area of gliosis noted in the right parietal lobe.
Area of hyperintensity on T2 and FLAIR noted in the right parietal and left frontal subcortical white matter suggestive of ischemic changes.

The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.

The imaged cranial nerves are also normal.

The cerebellum and cerebellar peduncles are also normal.

The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

MR angiography of the neck and in the cranial vessels shows bilateral normal common carotid, internal carotid, middle cerebral, anterior cerebral, Vertebral, basilar and posterior cerebral arteries.  No significant stenosis or occlusion noted.

IMPRESSION:
1. Chronic hemorrhage with areas of gliosis in the left frontal lobe.
2. White matter ischaemic changes in right parietal lobe and left frontal region.
3. No residual arterio venous malformation.
```

```text
C.T.SCAN OF BRAIN (FOLLOW UP)

As compared to previous CT done on 09/10/2011 present scan shows increase in hydrocephalus.
The third ventricle now measures 1.1 cms in maximum diameter.
Post contrast study shows an AVM in the region of right sided basal ganglia, head of caudate nucleus and anterior limb of internal capsule.  It appears to be supplied from the branches of right middle and anterior cerebral arteries, and seen draining into vein of Galen. The nidus measures approximately 3.2 x 2.0 x 3.2cm (AP, trans, CC). An aneurysm is noted in the medial temporal lobe, adjacent to the temporal horn of the right lateral ventricle, in posterior aspect of the nidus.  There is a hyperdense clot adjacent to this aneurysm.
Another small aneurysm is noted near the anterior aspect of the third ventricle, probably from the feeding arterial branch of the ACA.

Impression :
Arterio Venous Malformation in the right basal ganglia region with at least 2 aneurysms, Deep venous system drainage, intraventricular haemorrhage and hydrocephalus as detailed.
Suggested DSA correlation.
```
