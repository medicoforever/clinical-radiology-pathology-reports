### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `2247`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Brain SWI/GRE`  

```text
ARAVIND EYE HOSPITAL  (M.R.I. SCAN) of 17.08.2011:
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique : T1, T2 coronal, T2, FLAIR axial, DWI and SWI 

1.2 x 1.0 cm sized focal lesion is noted in right gangliocapsular region appearing hyperintense in T2 with exvacuo dilatation of adjacent right lateral ventricle. On susceptibility weighted imaging peripheral rim of blooming is noted suggestive of gliotic change secondary to resolving hematoma. No restriction is noted in diffusion weighted sequence to suggest infarct. In view of absence of heterogenicity of lesion underlying cavernoma is ruled out. 

Few scattered areas of subcortical and deep white matter hyperintensities of unknown clinical importance is noted in both cerebral hemispheres.

The rest of the neural parenchyma appears normal.

No evidence of bilateral retrobulbar neuritis.

Ventricles are normal.

Basal cisterns and cortical sulci are normal.

IMPRESSION: 

Gliotic focus in right gangliocapsular region with peripheral rim of blooming probably secondary to resolving hematoma - needs clinical correlation.
```
