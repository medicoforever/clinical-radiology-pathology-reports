### Case #02 — BEFUND_UBEID: `4088`
**Imaging Modality Context:** `MRI Brain + Contrast, MR Spectroscopy, Perfusion MRI, CT Brain`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN (30.08.11)

Technique: FLAIR axial, T1 3D, T2 coronal, DWI, SWI, Contrast - T1 3D spect.

A large illdefined heterogeneous signal intensity mass lesion measuring 6 x 4 x 6cm (AP x CC x Trans) noted in the right temporal lobe (on both sides of temporal horn of right lateral ventricle),
right thalamocapsular region with extension into right half of brainstem.  The lesion has both solid and cystic components.  The cystic components are located on the lateral aspect of the mass lesion. With DWI, restricted diffusion is noted in the solid component of the lesion in right thalamocapsular region and right half of brainstem.  On SWI, few tiny blooming foci noted within the mass lesion suggestive of haemorrhagic foci.  Perilesional white matter hyperintensity noted in right temporal and right basifrontal lobe in FLAIR images suggestive of oedema. 

Mass effect is seen in the form of compression over the right lateral ventricle and mild dilatation of left lateral ventricles.  Midline shift to left side measuring about 7mm is noted.  Impending right uncal herniation is also noted.

With contrast the lateral cystic component shows peripheral enhancement.  The medial solid component in right half of brainstem shows homogeneous enhancement.  The solid component in right medial temporal lobe shows no enhancement.  No definite evidence of meningeal / subependymal enhancement.On spectroscopy, elevated choline is noted in both enhancing and nonenhancing solid components.

MR angiography revealed no abnormality.

IMPRESSION: Heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion with both solid and cystic                 
                             components in right temporal lobe extending into brainstem causing
                             mass effect as detailed.  Features suggestive of high grade glioma.
Suggested         : Histopathological correlation.
```
