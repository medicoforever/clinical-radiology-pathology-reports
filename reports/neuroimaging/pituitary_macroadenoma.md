### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `822`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Sella / Brain`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: FLAIR Axial, T2 Cor, T1 pre and post contrast MPRAGE. 

Known case of pituitary macro adenoma status postoperative.

The brain parenchyma is normal in signal intensity pattern and morphology and displays a normal gray-white matter differentiation.
The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.
The periventricular and deep white matter display normal volume and signals.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.
The cerebellum and cerebellar peduncles are also normal.
The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.

There is a relatively well-defined mass seen in the sella and suprasellar region measuring approximately 3.4 x 2.2 x 2.7cm in its maximum AP, transverse, craniocaudal dimension.  The pituitary gland and infundibulum are not seen separately from the mass.  The mass shows patchy postcontrast enhancement.  Superiorly the mass displaces the optic chiasma and indents the floor of the third ventricle.  The mass closely abutts the medial wall of the right internal carotid artery, however there is no evidence of any encasement.  There is no evidence of extension into the cavernous sinus on both sides.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.

IMPRESSION: Known case of the pituitary macroadenoma status postoperative.  Present scan shows evidence of residual tumour as detailed.
```
