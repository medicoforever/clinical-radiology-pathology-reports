### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `7084`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI Brain`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN- 16.9.11

Technique: MPR T1, FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, DWI, SWI, 3D TOF MRA.

Bilateral cerebral and cerebellar atrophy noted.

T2 and FLAIR hyperintensity which shows diffusion restriction noted in right half of lower pons.

CSF intensity focus with minimal hyperintensity noted on both sides of pons superior to the previous lesion suggestive of chronic lacunar infarct.

Chronic lacunar infarct also noted in right caudate, right centrum semiovale, left thalamus.

T2 and FLAIR hyperintensity with no diffusion restriction seen in bilateral periventricular and frontoparietal white matter suggestive of ischemic changes.

The imaged cranial nerves are also normal.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.
 
MR angiography performed with 3D TOF technique showed normal both internal
carotid, anterior cerebral, middle cerebral, posterior cerebral, basilar and P.com arteries.

IMPRESSION:

1.	Acute right pontine infarct as detailed.
2.	Chronic lacunar infarcts with white matter ischemic changes as detailed.
```
