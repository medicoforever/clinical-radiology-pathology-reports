Clinical details:  Patient known case of the left jugular foramina schwannoma, status postoperative.  On follow-up.

Technique: MPR T1, FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, DWI, SWI, post contrast T1.

Well defined heterogeneous lesion is noted in the region of the left cerebellar pontine angle with extension into the jugular foramina.
The entire lesion measures 3.7 X 2.3 X 3.3 cm (anteroposterior, craniocaudal, transverse dimension).
The cisternal component is multiloculated lesion with few solid areas within.
Fluid-fluid level noted with some of the areas appearing hypointense on T2 and hyperintense on T1 suggestive of haemorrhage.  These areas show blooming on susceptibility weighted images.

The walls of the cysts show enhancement.  Solid areas show intense enhancement.

The foraminal component is predominantly solid and shows intense enhancement with contrast.

There is mass effect over the lower pons and mid brain with compression over the fourth ventricle.  There is mass effect over the left cerebellar hemisphere.

The left jugular foramina is compressed and laterally displaced by the lesion.  However normal contrast enhancement noted.

Rest of the dural sinuses appear normal.

No obvious dilatation of the third and lateral ventricles noted.

Rest of brain parenchyma appears normal.  

IMPRESSION: Known case of left jugular schwannoma.

Heterogeneous mass lesion noted in the region of left CP angle with extension into the jugular foramina with mass effect as detailed.
Mild increase in the size of the foraminal component as compared to the previous scan.
