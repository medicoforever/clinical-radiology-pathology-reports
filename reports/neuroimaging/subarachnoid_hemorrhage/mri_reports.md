### Case #001 (BEFUND_UBEID: `13703`)
- **Study Date:** `2011-10-19 08:04:13`
- **Modality Code:** `A-MRI`
- **Clinical Indication:** `MRI BRAIN WITH MRA`

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: MPR T1, FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, DWI, SWI, 3D TOF MRA,MRV

 Absence of flow signal noted in the superior sagittal sinus excluding the posterior 1/4th  suggestive of dural venous sinus thrombosis. An  area of haemorrhage, predominately involving the grey and white matter with areas of subarachnoid haemorrhage noted involving the lateral convexity of left frontal lobe with perilesional white matter hyperintensity suggestive of oedema is noted.  Mass effect in the form of midline shift to  right for a segment of 1.1 cm noted.  Compression over the left lateral  ventricle is also noted.  Hypoplastic transverse sinus is noted on left side..  Rest of the dural venous sinuses appears normal. 
 Small CSF density focus measuring about 1.1 X 0.8 cm is noted involving the left superior cerebellar hemisphere suggestive of gliosis. Rest of neuroparenchyma appears normal.

The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.

The periventricular and deep white matter display normal volume and signals.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.

The imaged cranial nerves are also normal.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.


IMPRESSION:

Features suggestive of dural venous sinus thrombosis involving superior sagittal sinus with intraparenchymal hemorrhage and mass effect in the left frontal lobe as detailed.
```
