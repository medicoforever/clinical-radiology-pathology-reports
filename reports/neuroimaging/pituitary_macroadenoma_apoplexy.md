### Case #01 — BEFUND_UBEID: `813`
**Imaging Modality Context:** `Dedicated Sella MRI (Dynamic Contrast, Coronal T1/T2)`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: FLAIR Axial, T2 Cor, T1 pre and post contrast MPRAGE. 

The brain parenchyma is normal in signal intensity pattern and morphology and displays a normal gray-white matter differentiation.
The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.
The periventricular and deep white matter display normal volume and signals.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.
The cerebellum and cerebellar peduncles are also normal.
The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.

There is a relatively well-defined mass seen in the sella and suprasellar region measuring approximately 2.3 x 2.2 x 2.9cm in its maximum AP, transverse, craniocaudal dimension.  The pituitary gland and infundibulum is not seen separately from the mass.  The mass appears predominantly hyperintense on T1 and T2-weighted images, and shows thin rim of peripheral enhancement on post contrast sequences.  Superiorly the mass displaces the optic chiasma and indents the floor of the third ventricle.  The mass closely about the medial wall of the right internal carotid artery, however thee is no evidence of any encasement.  There is no evidence of extension into the cavernous sinus on both sides.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.
There is opacification of the right maxillary sinus with mucosal thickening in the left maxillary sinus.

IMPRESSION: Sellar and suprasellar mass as detailed.  Features may represent a pituitary macroadenoma with apoplexy.
```
