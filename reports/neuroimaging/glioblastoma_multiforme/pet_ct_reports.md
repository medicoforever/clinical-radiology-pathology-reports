WHOLE BODY PET CT SCAN
Clinical Indication: Glioblastoma multiforme ,post surgery and RT - for residual disease evaluation.
Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid-thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. A blood sugar levels were checked and was 96mg/dl.

Findings:
BRAIN:
Intensely hypermetabolic heterogeneous lesion with cystic and minimally enhancing solid areas within, noted in right temporal region, measuring 6.5x5.1x4.0cm (APxTRxCC) with an SUV max of 16.1 and  minimal surrounding edema.
Post -operative changes in the right fronto-parietal and temporal region. 

HEAD and NECK: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
There is no significant cervical or supraclavicular adenopathy. The pharyngeal mucosal space, larynx and the cervical trachea are unremarkable. The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.

CHEST:
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the chest.
Subsegmental atelectasis medial segment of the right middle lobe, patchy ground glass haziness in posterior basal segment of left lower lobe.
No abnormalities are detected in breast, bilateral axilla or internal mammary node.

ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the abdomen and pelvis.
Uterine fibroid noted, measuring 2.0x1.5cm in left antero-lateral wall.
Liver parenchyma appears normal. Kidneys appear unremarkable. There is no hydroureteronephrosis .The gallbladder, spleen; pancreas and the adrenals are unremarkable on this contrast study. There is no free fluid or loculated collection in the abdomen and pelvis. There is no significant retroperitoneal, mesenteric or pelvic adenopathy.

SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the extremities and musculo- skeletal system. There is no focal suspicious lytic or blastic osseous lesion.

IMPRESSION:
RESIDUAL MASS LESION IN THE RIGHT TEMPORAL LOBE WITH INTENSELY HYPER METABOLIC SOLID AREAS AND CYSTIC DEGENERATION; CONSISTENT WITH RESIDUAL ACTIVE DISEASE.
NO DEFINITE EVIDENCE OF METABOLICALLY ACTIVE DISEASE ELSEWHERE IN THE WHOLE BODY SURVEY.
