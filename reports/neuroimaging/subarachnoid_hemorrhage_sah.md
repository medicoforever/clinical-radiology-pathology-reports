Fracture of body and spinous process of right scapula.

Cervical spine : There is no evidence of fracture seen.  
Facetal arthropathy at C1 to C2 on the left side noted.
Straightening noted.  Sclerotic focus noted at D1 vertebra - possibly bony island.

IMPRESSION: 1. Right frontal extradural hematoma with adjacent subpial bleed.
                            2. Thin interhemispherical bleed along posterior aspect.

                            4. Sigmoid sinus and right internal jugular vein thrombus.
                            5. Fractures as detailed.
