C.T.SCAN OF BRAIN

Serial axial sections of the brain were studied from the base to the vertex.

Intraparenchymal bleed measuring 1.6 X 0.8 cm is noted in the left temporal lobe with minimal perilesional edema.  

Minimal Subarchnoid hemorrhage is noted in the left temporal lobe and occipital lobe.

Bilateral subcortical and deep white matter ischemic changes are seen. 

The C.P.Angles and posterior fossa contents are normal.

The ventricular system shows normal anatomical configuration.

There is no midline shift seen.

The basal cisterns, cortical sulci and sylvian fissures are normal.

The sella, parasellar and suprasellar regions are normal.

On contrast administration intracranial arteries shows normal enhancement. Dural venous sinus thrombosis involving the superior sagittal sinus, left transverse sinus, sigmoid sinus and visualised portion of the  internal jugular vein. Right transverse sinus, sigmoid sinus and visualised portion of the  internal jugular vein shows normal opacification.

IMPRESSION: 
1.  Intraparenchymal bleed in the left temporal lobe.
2.  Minimal subarachnoid haemorrhage in left temporal and occipital lobe.
3.  Dural venous sinus thrombosis involving  superior sagittal sinus, left transverse sinus, sigmoid sinus and IJV as detailed.

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `5901`)
**Modality Context:** `CT / CTA Brain`

C.T TRAUMA BRAIN (BRAIN+C.SPINE)  (C.T. SCAN) of 13.09.2011:

C.T. SCAN OF TRAUMA BRAIN AND CERVICAL SPINE

Serial axial sections of the brain and cervical spine were studied.

Hemorrhagic contusion involving right temporal lobe. Left temporal lobe shows contusion with focal parenchymal edema.
Minimal subarachnoid hemorrhage noted in the left frontal convexity.

The C.P.Angles and posterior fossa contents are normal.

The ventricular system shows normal anatomical configuration.

There is no midline shift seen.

The basal cisterns, cortical sulci and sylvian fissures are normal.

The sella, parasellar and suprasellar regions are normal.

Linear fracture without displacement noted involving left parieto temporal bone, extending into left greater wing of sphenoid and lateral wall of left orbit. Linear fracture also noted involving left zygomatic arch.

Cervical spine shows severe degenerative changes with multiple tiny lytic lesions. Loss of cervical lordosis with bony ankylosis of C5,C6 and C7 vertebral bodies seen.

No abnormal pre or paravertebral soft tissue seen.

IMPRESSION: 
Bilateral temporal lobe contusion with hemorrhagic componenet on right side.
Minimal left frontal subarachnoid hemorrhage.
Fracture of left parieto temporal bone, extending into left greater wing of sphenoid and lateral wall of left orbit. Linear fracture also noted involving left zygomatic arch.
Severe degenerative changes of cervical spine with multiple tiny lytic lesions and  bony ankylosis of C5,C6 and C7 vertebral bodies.
