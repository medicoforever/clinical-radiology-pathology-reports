MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: MPR T1, T2 Coronal, FLAIR Axial, SPACE T2 -IAC, DWI.
 
Chronic infarct with gliosis is seen in the left inferior cerebellar hemisphere.

Rest of the neuroparenchyma is normal.

The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.

The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.

The internal auditory canal is normal. The seventh nd eighth cranial nerves are normal. The cerebello pontine angles are normal. The inner ear structures are normal.

MRA - hpoplastic P1 segment of right PCA. Rest of the vessels is normal.

IMPRESSION:

 1. Chronic infarct in the left inferior cerebellar hemosphere.
 2. No other abnormality detected.

1.	Acute right pontine infarct as detailed.
2.	Chronic lacunar infarcts with white matter ischemic changes as detailed.
