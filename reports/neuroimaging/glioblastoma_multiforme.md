### Case Report #1 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1100`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / CT Brain`  

```text
BRAIN MRI REPORT - 04.08.2011:
The large well-defined heterogeneous signal intensity mass lesion measuring 6.4x 4.4 X 4.3 cm in noted in the occipital lobe and the adjacent temporal lobe, parasagittal in location.  The lesion appears predominantly isointense to grey matter in all sequences.  the lesion shows central hypointense areas suggestive of necrosis. With contrast, the lesion shows irregular  peripheral nodular enhancement with central nonenhancing areas.  The adjacent white matter in the left parieto-occipital temporal lobe shows hyperintense signal intensity on FLAIR sequences suggestive of perilesional  white matter oedema. the lesion noted on both sides of the occipital horn of the left lateral ventricle.  subependymal enhancement is also noted on the posterior most aspect of the occipital horn of the left lateral ventricle and the rest of the occipital horn appears to draps  along the superolateral aspect of the mass lesion. on susceptibility weighted images, few prominent vascular channels are noted within the lesion.  With spectroscopic, elevated choline  Is noted within the enhancing areas.  The white matter adjacent to the lesion on the anterolateral aspect of the mass lesion (for about 4mm) shows elevated choline  suggestive of peri tumour  infiltration. Mass effect in  the form of effacement of the adjacent cortical sulci noted in the left cerebral hemisphere.
No evidence of any dural venous sinus thrombosis noted.
No other significant abnormality detected in the rest of the  neuro parenchyma.
Impression;
The large well-defined mass lesion  with irregular peripheral nodular enhancement involving the occipital and temporal lobes on the left side with mass effect and extensions  as detailed-
Features suggestive of glioblastoma multiforme. needs histopathology correlation.
```

### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `4378`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI / CT Brain`  

```text
C.T.SCAN OF BRAIN -2.9.11

Serial axial sections of the brain were studied from the base to the vertex before and after administration of IV contrast.

Known case of right frontoparietal glioblastoma multiformae, status post op, radio and chemotherapy.

Present scan shows heterogeneously enhancing mass lesion with irregular peripheral rim enhancement and enhancing solid component with central non enhancing area suggestive of necrosis in the right frontal lobe.

No evidence of haemorrhage or calcification seen within the lesion.

Extensive white matter oedema noted involving the entire right cerebral hemisphere.

Mass effect is noted in the form of subfalcine herniation to the left, midline shift to the left measuring 8.0mm and mild uncal herniation on the right.

Partial compression of the right lateral ventricle with dilatation of the left lateral ventricle.

The sella, parasellar and suprasellar regions are normal.

Right parietal craniotomy changes noted - status post op.

Mucosal thickening noted in right maxillary sinus and ethmoid sinus.

IMPRESSION:  

Known case of right frontoparietal GBM, status post op, post RT and post chemotherapy.
As compared to previous C.T.scan dated 15.7.11 (plain scan), present scan shows increase in size of the lesion and perilesional oedema and mass effect  as detailed.
```
