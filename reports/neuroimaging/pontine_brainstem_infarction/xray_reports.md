### Case #001 (BEFUND_UBEID: `26597`)
- **Study Date:** `2011-12-10 11:13:14`
- **Modality Code:** `A-XRAY`
- **Clinical Indication:** `,,`

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique: MPR T1, FLAIR Axial, T2 Coronal, DWI, SWI, 3D TOF MRA.

T2 and FLAIR hyperintensity involving the pons, predominately in the right side which shows diffusion restriction of diffusion weighted images- suggestive of acute infarct.  Areas of blooming on susceptibility weighted images noted in the right side of pontine infarct - suggestive of haemorrhage.

Few small hyperintensity of are noted involving bilateral cerebellar hemisphere and left thalamus which shows diffusion restriction on diffusion weighted images - suggestive of acute infarct.
Rest of the brain parenchyma is normal in signal intensity pattern and morphology and displays a normal gray-white matter differentiation.

The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.

The periventricular and deep white matter display normal volume and signals.

The midbrain and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.

The imaged cranial nerves are also normal.

The cerebellar peduncles are also normal.

The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.
MRA- Absence of low signal is noted within the intracranial segment (V4) of right vertebral artery and the basilar artery - suggestive of thrombosis.
The A1 and A2 segment of right anterior cerebral artery is hypoplastic.  Rest of the arteries show normal flow signal within.
Mucosal thickening is noted in all the paranasal sinuses- suggestive of sinusitis.

IMPRESSION:
1)	Acute infarct with small areas of haemorrhagic focus involving the pons (RT>LT).
2)	Acute small infarct involving the bilateral cerebellar hemisphere and left thalamus.
3)	Absence of flow signal within the intracranial segment (V4) of right vertebral artery and the basilar artery - suggestive of thrombosis.
```
