FLAIR sequence.  Diffusion restriction is seen in the lesion in the DWI. No abnormal enhancement of the lesion is seen in the present study.

The lesion causes significant mass effect over the adjacent structures causing rotation of the brain stem and mild compression of the fourth ventricle.

The lesion pushes the right trigeminal nerve to the superior medial position.  Mild T2 hyperintensity is seen in the cisternal part of the trigeminal nerve near the compressed part.

The right eighth nerve appears to be draped over the lesion in the posterolateral aspect.
The right facial nerve could not be clearly delineated in the CPA region. Encasement of the nerve by the mass lesion can't be ruled out.
The right superior cerebellar artery appears to be draped over the lesion in the superomedial aspect.

Few small scattered punctuate T2 hyper intensities are seen in the bilateral white matter, more in the right frontal lobe.

The brain parenchyma is normal in signal intensity pattern and morphology and displays a normal gray-white matter differentiation.

The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals except the mass effect by the lesion.

The imaged cranial nerves on the left side appear normal.

The cerebellum and cerebellar peduncles are also normal except the mass effect by the lesion.

The ventricular system is normal in size and appearance.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.

Features are suggestive of epidermoid in the right CPA region causing mass effect over the nerves and vessels as detailed.

Displacement and mild effacement of the fourth ventricle seen.  No evidence of hydrocephalous.  Ventricular drainage tube noted in situ, tip in the body of the right lateral ventricle.

Another extraaxial dural based enhancing lesion measuring 9.0 X 5.4 mm noted in the right frontal region, possibly meningioma.

The periventricular and deep white matter display tiny FLAIR and T2 hyperintensities suggesting ischemic changes.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprasellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal. Prominent CSF space noted in bilateral optic nerve sheath complex.

Known case of right acoustic neuroma, Status post operative.
