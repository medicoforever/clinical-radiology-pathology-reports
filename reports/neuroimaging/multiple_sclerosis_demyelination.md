IMPRESSION:
"	 LONG SEGMENT SUBTLE HYPERINTENSITY INVOLVING THE SPINAL CORD WITH SUBTLE CONTRAST ENHANCEMENT AND FOCAL LESION IN THE SPLENIUM OF CORPUS CALLOSUM- POSSIBILITY OF DEMYELINATING  DISEASE TO BE CONSIDERED.
