### Case #001 (BEFUND_UBEID: `24748`)
- **Study Date:** `2011-12-02 16:06:11`
- **Modality Code:** `A-NM`
- **Clinical Indication:** `,`

```text
REGIONAL PET CT SCAN 

Clinical Indication: - Nasopharyngeal Ca, post chemo And RT; for evaluation.
Technique:
Whole body images (base of skull of mid thigh) were acquired in 3-D mode 60 min after IV Injection of 370 MBq of 18F-FDG using a dedicated PET_CT scanner. Reconstruction of the acquired data was performed so as to obtain fused PET CT images in transaxial, coronal, and sagittal views. A blood sugar levels were checked and was 94 mg/dl.
Findings:
HEAD and NECK: 
Intense hyper metabolism is noted in increased soft tissue thickness in the posterior aspect of the nasopharynx, left lateral aspect of the oropharynx up to the tonsillar fossa with heterogenous contrast enhancement and an SUV max of 2.5 increasing to 4.5 on delayed imaging.
Para-pharyngeal space is normal.
Ametabolic bilateral level IB and II lymphnodes are noted, largest nodal short axis measures 0.6cm (insignificant by size criteria).
No other pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the region of the head and neck.
The parotid glands and submandibular glands are unremarkable. The mastoid air cells and the paranasal sinuses are unremarkable. No focal mass lesion/mass effect is seen within the visualized brain parenchyma.
CHEST:
Intense hyper metabolism is noted in apical pleural thickening with adjacent fibrosis and an SUV max of 2.8.
Multiple scattered centriacinar and nodular opacities noted in both lungs, few of the centriacinar nodules showing tree in bud appearance suggestive of infectious etiology.  
Intensely hypermetabolic left hilar lymph node with an SUV max of 3.2. 
Ametabolic pre-carinal, sub-carinal and aortopulmonary window lymphnodes are noted, largest nodal short axis measures 0.9cm in left aortopulmonary window (insignificant by size and metabolism criteria).
ABDOMEN AND PELVIS: 
Spleen shows heterogenous contrast enhancement with diffuse high grade FDG uptake.
SKELETAL: 
No pathological focus of FDG uptake noted in the visualised bones.
End plate sclerosis noted in lower dorsal and upper lumbar vertebrae.
IMPRESSION:
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC SOFT TISSUE THICKENING IN THE RIGHT POSTERIOR NASOPHARYNX CONSISTENT WITH RESIDUAL ACTIVE DISEASE.
INTENSELY HYPERMETABOLIC BILATERAL LUNG FIBROSIS AND NODULAR OPACITIES WITH HILAR LYMPHNODES; COULD BE INFECTIOUS IN NATURE.  
INCIDENTALLY NOTED DIFFUSELY HYPERMETABOLIC SPLEEN IS OF UNCERTAIN SIGNIFICANCE.

Chief Consultant NM          Consultant NM             Radiologist
```
