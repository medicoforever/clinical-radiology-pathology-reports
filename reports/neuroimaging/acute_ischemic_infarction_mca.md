### Case Report #2 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1006`)
**Modality Context:** `MRI DWI / CT Brain`  

```text
MRI SCAN OF BRAIN

Technique:FLAIR axial,T2 coronal,MPRAGE, DWI,MRA,MRV.

Age-related cerebral atrophy.
Hyperintensity is noted in the bilateral occipital lobe, right temporal lobe, left posteromedial temporal lobe and small hyperintensity in right cerebellar hemisphere.  These areas show diffusion restriction on diffusion weighted images.  Hyperintensity is noted involving the gyral region of bilateral occipital lobe-  suggestive of haemorrhagic transformation.
Gliotic changes are noted involving the right high frontoparietal region of  rt MCA territory.
The ventricles and sulci are mildly prominent.No evidence of midline shift.
MRA shows complete occlusion of the distal cervical,petrous and cavernous segment of right ICA. The right MCA and ACA shows normal signal intensity - suggestive of good cross circulation. Left ICA, MCA  ana ACA show normal signal intensity.
The mid segment of basilar artery shows severe luminal narrowing - may represent severe stenosis/dissection.
The deep gray matter and the internal capsules are symmetrical and normal in signal intensity.
The periventricular and deep white matter shows scattered areas of hyperintensity - suggestive of white matter ischaemic changes.  

The midbrain, pons and medulla are normal in morphology and signals.
The imaged cranial nerves are also normal.
The left cerebellar hemisphere and cerebellar peduncles are also normal.

The sella turcica is normal in size and shape and displays a normal pituitary gland and infundibulum. The suprastellar cistern and hypothalamic region are unremarkable for any pathology.

The orbits and their contents are grossly normal.

IMPRESSION:

"	INFARCT  INVOLVING THE BILATERAL  POSTERIOR CEREBRAL ARTERY TERRITORY REGION WITH HAEMORRHAGIC TRANSFORMATION IN THE BILATERAL OCCIPITAL LOBE.
"	AGE-RELATED CEREBRAL ATROPHY WITH CHRONIC INFARCT INVOLVING THE RIGHT MCA TERRITORY REGION.
"	CHRONIC INFARCT INVOLVING THE RIGHT RIGHT FRONTOPARIETAL REGION AND WHITE MATTER ISCHAEMIC CHANGES AS DETAILED.
"	COMPLETE OCCLUSION OF THE RIGHT ICA AND SEVERE LUMINAL NARROWING / DISSECTION INVOLVING THE MIDSEGMENT OF  THE BASILAR ARTERY - SUGGESTED DSA CORRELATION.
```

### Case Report #3 (BEFUND_UBEID: `1748`)

BRAIN (HEAD)  (C.T. SCAN) of 10.08.2011:

Serial axial sections of the brain were studied from the base to the vertex.

Diffuse cerebral and cerebellar atrophy for age.

Chronic infarct in the left frontal and parietal region involving both grey and white matter and left centrum semiovale with ex vacuo dilatation of the posterior aspect of the body of the left lateral ventricle.
  
The C.P.Angles and posterior fossa contents are normal.

The ventricular system shows normal anatomical configuration.

There is no midline shift seen.

The basal cisterns, cortical sulci and sylvian fissures are normal.

The sella, parasellar and suprasellar regions are normal.

IMPRESSION: 
 1. Diffuse cerebral and cerebellar atrophy for age.
 2.  Chronic left MCA territory infarct.
